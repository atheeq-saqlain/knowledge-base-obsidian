# Two Pointers

## Definition

An algorithmic pattern that advances two indices through a sequence (often from ends or at different speeds) to find pairs, partitions, or windows. Requires: [[Array]].

## Description

Common on sorted arrays and linked lists. Variants include opposite-direction pointers, same-direction slow/fast pointers, and partition pointers.
