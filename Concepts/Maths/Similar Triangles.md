# Similar Triangles

## Definition

Two triangles are similar if their corresponding angles are equal and their corresponding sides are in the same ratio (proportion). Similarity is written ΔABC ~ ΔDEF with vertices listed in corresponding order.

## Description

Equiangular triangles have equal corresponding angles; corresponding sides are then proportional. Correspondence of vertices matters: if ΔABC ~ ΔDEF, then ∠A = ∠D, ∠B = ∠E, ∠C = ∠F and AB/DE = BC/EF = CA/FD.

Criteria that check similarity with fewer conditions include AA, SSS, and SAS.
