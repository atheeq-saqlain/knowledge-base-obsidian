# Conversion of Solids

## Definition

When a solid is melted and recast into another shape (or earth from a well is spread as a platform), the volume remains unchanged: volume of the new solid equals volume of the original solid.

## Description

Equate volumes to find an unknown radius, height or number of pieces (coins, cones of ice cream, lead shots). Flowing water problems equate pipe/canal discharge volume to irrigated depth × area or to tank capacity.
