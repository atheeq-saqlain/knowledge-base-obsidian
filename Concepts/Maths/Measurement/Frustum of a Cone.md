# Frustum of a Cone

## Definition

A frustum of a cone is the solid left when a right circular cone is cut by a plane parallel to its base and the smaller cone is removed. With parallel radii r₁, r₂ and height h: slant height l = √(h² + (r₁ − r₂)²), CSA = π(r₁ + r₂)l, TSA = π(r₁ + r₂)l + πr₁² + πr₂², volume = (1/3)πh(r₁² + r₂² + r₁r₂).

## Description

Glasses, buckets and funnels are often frustums. An open container omits the top circle from the metal-sheet area. Related to [[Volume of Combination of Solids]] when a frustum is part of a larger solid.
