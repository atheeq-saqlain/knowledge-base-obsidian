# Surface Area of Combination of Solids

## Definition

When solids are joined, faces that meet are no longer exposed. The total surface area of the combined solid is the sum of the curved (and remaining flat) surface areas of the parts that stay exposed.

## Description

Typical joins: cone on a hemisphere (TSA = 2πr² + πrl), cube surmounted by a hemisphere (add CSA of hemisphere and subtract the covered circle), cylinder with hemispherical ends (2πrh + 4πr²). Always subtract interfaces and add only outer surfaces. Uses CSA/TSA of cylinder, cone, sphere and hemisphere.
