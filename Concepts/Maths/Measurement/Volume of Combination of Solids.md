# Volume of Combination of Solids

## Definition

The volume of a solid made by joining two or more solids equals the sum of the volumes of its parts (internal space is not lost at the join).

## Description

Examples: cone on a hemisphere, cylinder with conical ends, cuboid with conical depressions (subtract cavity volumes). When a solid is melted and recast, volume is conserved ([[Conversion of Solids]]). Standard part formulas: cylinder πr²h, cone (1/3)πr²h, sphere (4/3)πr³, hemisphere (2/3)πr³.
