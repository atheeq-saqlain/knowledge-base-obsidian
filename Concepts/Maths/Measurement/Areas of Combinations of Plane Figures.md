# Areas of Combinations of Plane Figures

## Definition

Find a required region by adding or subtracting areas of familiar shapes — squares, rectangles, triangles, full circles, semicircles, sectors, and segments — according to how the figure is built.

## Description

Typical patterns: square minus inscribed circles; semicircles on the sides of a square; concentric sectors (ring sector); racing track = outer path minus inner path; overlapping quadrants (leaf). Always identify the shaded set as a sum or difference before substituting formulas for circumference, sector, or segment areas.
