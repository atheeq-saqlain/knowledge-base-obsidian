# Circumference and Area of a Circle

## Definition

For a circle of radius r, the circumference (perimeter) is 2πr and the area is πr². The ratio of circumference to diameter is the constant π (irrational; in school work take π ≈ 22/7 or 3.14).

## Description

Circumference = πd = 2πr. Cutting a circle into many thin sectors and rearranging them suggests a rectangle of sides πr and r, whose area is πr². Applications: fencing and ploughing costs, wheel revolutions (distance = revolutions × circumference), combining circumferences or areas of two circles into one equivalent circle, concentric scoring rings (annuli).
