# Area of a Segment

## Definition

Area of a [[Segment of a Circle]] equals the area of the corresponding [[Sector of a Circle]] minus the area of the triangle formed by the two radii and the chord.

## Description

Compute sector area with Area of a Sector, then subtract (1/2)r² sin θ or use right-triangle geometry when θ = 90°. Major segment = area of circle − minor segment.
