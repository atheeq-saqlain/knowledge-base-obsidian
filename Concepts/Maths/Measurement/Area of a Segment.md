# Area of a Segment

## Definition

Area of a minor [[Segment of a Circle]] equals the area of the corresponding sector minus the area of the triangle formed by the two radii and the chord.

## Description

Sector area uses [[Area of a Sector]]. Triangle area: for θ = 90°, (1/2)r²; for θ = 60°, equilateral so (√3/4)r²; in general (1/2)r² sin θ (or drop a perpendicular from the centre to the chord). Major segment = πr² − minor segment. Used for grazing sectors, table-cover designs, and shaded regions bounded by a chord and an arc.
