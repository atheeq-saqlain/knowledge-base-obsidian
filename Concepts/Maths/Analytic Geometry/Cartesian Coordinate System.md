# Cartesian Coordinate System

## Definition

In the plane, each point has an ordered pair (x, y): the abscissa x is the signed distance from the y-axis, and the ordinate y is the signed distance from the x-axis. Points on the x-axis have the form (x, 0); points on the y-axis have the form (0, y).

## Description

Coordinate geometry studies figures by translating geometric questions into algebra (and conversely). The origin O(0, 0) is the intersection of the axes. Quadrants are labelled I–IV in the usual counterclockwise order from the positive x-axis.
