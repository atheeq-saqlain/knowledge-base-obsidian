# Distance Formula

## Definition

The distance between P(x₁, y₁) and Q(x₂, y₂) is √[(x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²]. The distance of P(x, y) from the origin O(0, 0) is √(x² + y²). Derived from the [[Pythagoras Theorem]] on the horizontal and vertical legs of the right triangle formed by P, Q, and (x₂, y₁).

## Description

Drop perpendiculars from the points to the axes (or draw the right triangle with legs |x₂ − x₁| and |y₂ − y₁|). Then PQ² equals the sum of the squares of those legs. Used to test collinearity (AB + BC = AC), classify triangles and quadrilaterals by side and diagonal lengths, and locate points equidistant from two given points.
