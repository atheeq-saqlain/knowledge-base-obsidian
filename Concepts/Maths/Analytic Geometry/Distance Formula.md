# Distance Formula

## Definition

The distance between P(x₁, y₁) and Q(x₂, y₂) is √[(x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²]. The distance of P(x, y) from the origin is √(x² + y²).

## Description

Derived from Pythagoras Theorem on the horizontal and vertical legs. Used for collinearity checks, classifying quadrilaterals, and finding points equidistant from two given points.

Example: distance between (2, 3) and (4, 1) is √8 = 2√2.
