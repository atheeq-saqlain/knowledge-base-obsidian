# Experimental Probability

## Definition

The experimental (empirical) probability of an event E is P(E) = (number of trials in which E happened) / (total number of trials).

## Description

It is an estimate from performed experiments and can vary slightly under identical conditions. As the number of trials grows large, the experimental probability tends to approach the [[Theoretical Probability]].
