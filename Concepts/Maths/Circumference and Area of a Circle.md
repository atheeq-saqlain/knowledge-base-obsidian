# Circumference and Area of a Circle

## Definition

For a circle of radius r, the circumference is 2πr and the area is πr² (π ≈ 22/7 or 3.14 for calculations).

## Description

Circumference is the perimeter of the circle. Practical problems often find r from fencing cost or revolutions of a wheel, then compute area.
