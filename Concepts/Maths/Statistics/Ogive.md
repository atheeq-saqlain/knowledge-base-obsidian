# Ogive

## Definition

An ogive is the graph of a cumulative frequency distribution. A less-than ogive plots upper class limits against less-than cumulative frequencies; a more-than ogive plots lower class limits against more-than cumulative frequencies.

## Description

The median can be read as the abscissa of the intersection of the two ogives, or by drawing a horizontal line from n/2 to the less-than ogive and dropping a perpendicular to the horizontal axis. Related to [[Median of Grouped Data]].
