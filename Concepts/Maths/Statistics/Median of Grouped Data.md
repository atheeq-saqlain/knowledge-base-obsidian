# Median of Grouped Data

## Definition

Form a less-than cumulative frequency table. Let n = Σfᵢ and find the median class (first class whose cf ≥ n/2). Then
Median = l + ((n/2 − cf)/f) · h,
where l is the lower limit of the median class, cf the cumulative frequency before it, f its frequency, and h the class size.

## Description

The median is the middle value of the ordered data. For continuous inclusive classes, adjust boundaries (e.g. 118–126 → 117.5–126.5) before applying the formula. Graphically, the median is the x-coordinate where the less-than and more-than ogives meet, or where cf = n/2 on a less-than ogive.
