# Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic

## Definition

Every composite number can be factorised as a product of primes, and this factorisation is unique apart from the order of the prime factors.

## Description

32760 = 2³ × 3² × 5 × 7 × 13. Used to find HCF (lowest powers of common primes) and LCM (highest powers). Also underpins proofs that √p is irrational when p is prime.

Verify: LCM × HCF = product of two numbers (for two integers).
