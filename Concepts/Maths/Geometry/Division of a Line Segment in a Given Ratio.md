# Division of a Line Segment in a Given Ratio

## Definition

To divide a line segment AB internally in the positive integer ratio m : n with ruler and compass: draw a ray AX making an acute angle with AB; mark m + n equal steps on AX; join the last point to B; through the mth point draw a line parallel to that join meeting AB at C. Then AC : CB = m : n. Justified by the [[Basic Proportionality Theorem]] (and equivalently by [[AA Similarity Criterion]]).

## Description

Equal steps on the ray give AAₘ / AₘAₘ₊ₙ = m/n; the parallel transfers that ratio onto AB. An alternative method draws a second ray BY ∥ AX from B, marks m equal steps on AX and n on BY, and joins the last points; AA similarity of the resulting triangles again yields AC/BC = m/n.
