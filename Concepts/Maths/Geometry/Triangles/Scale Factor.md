# Scale Factor

## Definition

The scale factor (or representative fraction) of two [[Similar Figures]] is the constant ratio of the lengths of corresponding sides.

## Description

If corresponding sides of similar polygons are in the ratio k : 1, the linear scale factor is k. Corresponding lengths scale by k; areas of similar plane figures scale by k². In constructions, a scale factor such as 2/3 means the new figure’s corresponding sides are two-thirds those of the original.
