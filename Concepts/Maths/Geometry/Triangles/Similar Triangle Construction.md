# Similar Triangle Construction

## Definition

To construct a triangle similar to a given triangle with a given scale factor k, mark points along a ray to create the ratio k (via parallel lines), then draw parallels to the sides of the original triangle to obtain the scaled copy.

## Description

Scale factor k = (side of new triangle)/(corresponding side of given triangle). For k < 1 the new triangle is smaller; for k > 1 it is larger. Relies on AA Similarity Criterion and division of a line segment in a given ratio.

Example: triangle with sides 2/3 of a 4, 5, 6 cm triangle.
