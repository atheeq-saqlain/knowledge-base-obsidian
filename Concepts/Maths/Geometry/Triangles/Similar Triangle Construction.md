# Similar Triangle Construction

## Definition

To construct a triangle similar to a given ΔABC with scale factor m/n: from B draw a ray BX opposite A; mark max(m, n) equal steps on BX; join the denominator step to C; through the numerator step draw a parallel to that join meeting BC (or its extension) at C′; through C′ draw a parallel to CA meeting BA (or its extension) at A′. Then ΔA′BC′ ∼ ΔABC with corresponding sides in the ratio m : n. Uses [[Division of a Line Segment in a Given Ratio]] and [[AA Similarity Criterion]].

## Description

Scale factor k = (side of new triangle)/(corresponding side of given triangle). If k < 1 (for example 3/4 or 2/3), C′ lies on BC and the new triangle is smaller. If k > 1 (for example 5/3 or 7/5), extend BC and BA so C′ and A′ lie beyond C and A. Parallels create equal corresponding angles, so the new triangle is similar to the given one.
