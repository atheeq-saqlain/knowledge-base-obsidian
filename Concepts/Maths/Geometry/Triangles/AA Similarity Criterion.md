# AA Similarity Criterion

## Definition

If two angles of one triangle are respectively equal to two angles of another triangle, then the triangles are [[Similar Triangles]] (AA). Equivalently, if all three corresponding angles are equal (AAA), the triangles are similar and corresponding sides are proportional.

## Description

Because the third angle is determined by the angle sum of a triangle, two equal angles suffice. Write the similarity with correct vertex order, e.g. ΔABC ~ ΔPQR when ∠A = ∠P and ∠B = ∠Q.

Often used with parallel lines (corresponding or alternate angles), a shared (common) angle, or right triangles that share an acute angle — for example lamp-post and shadow problems, or a pole and a tower casting shadows at the same time.
