# Similar Figures

## Definition

Two geometric figures are similar if they have the same shape but not necessarily the same size. All congruent figures are similar, but similar figures need not be congruent.

## Description

All circles are similar; all squares are similar; all equilateral triangles are similar. A circle and a square are not similar. A rhombus of side 1.5 cm and a square of side 3 cm are not similar, because corresponding angles need not match.

Two polygons with the same number of sides are similar if (i) their corresponding angles are equal and (ii) their corresponding sides are in the same ratio (proportional). Neither condition alone is enough. The constant ratio of corresponding sides is the scale factor (also called the representative fraction).
