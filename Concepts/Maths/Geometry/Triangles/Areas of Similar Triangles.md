# Areas of Similar Triangles

## Definition

The ratio of the areas of two [[Similar Triangles]] equals the square of the ratio of their corresponding sides.

## Description

If ΔABC ~ ΔPQR, then ar(ABC)/ar(PQR) = (AB/PQ)² = (BC/QR)² = (CA/RP)².

Example: areas 64 cm² and 121 cm² with EF = 15.4 cm and ΔABC ~ ΔDEF ⇒ BC = 15.4 × (8/11) = 11.2 cm.
