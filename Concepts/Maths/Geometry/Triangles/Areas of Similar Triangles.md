# Areas of Similar Triangles

## Definition

The ratio of the areas of two [[Similar Triangles]] equals the square of the ratio of their corresponding sides.

## Description

If ΔABC ~ ΔPQR, then ar(ABC)/ar(PQR) = (AB/PQ)² = (BC/QR)² = (CA/RP)². The same square relation holds for corresponding medians, altitudes, and other linear elements.

If the areas of two similar triangles are equal, the side ratio is 1, so the triangles are congruent. Example: areas 64 cm² and 121 cm² with EF = 15.4 cm and ΔABC ~ ΔDEF ⇒ BC = 15.4 × (8/11) = 11.2 cm. Side ratio 4 : 9 ⇒ area ratio 16 : 81.
