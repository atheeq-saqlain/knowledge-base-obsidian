# Converse of Basic Proportionality Theorem

## Definition

If a line divides any two sides of a triangle in the same ratio, then the line is parallel to the third side.

## Description

In ΔABC, if D and E lie on AB and AC respectively and AD/DB = AE/EC, then DE ∥ BC. This is the converse of the [[Basic Proportionality Theorem]].

Use it to decide whether EF ∥ QR from given segment lengths, and to prove that the line joining the mid-points of two sides of a triangle is parallel to the third side.
