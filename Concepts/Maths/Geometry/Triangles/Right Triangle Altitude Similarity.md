# Right Triangle Altitude Similarity

## Definition

If a perpendicular is drawn from the right-angled vertex of a right triangle to the hypotenuse, then the two triangles on either side of the perpendicular are [[Similar Triangles]] to each other and to the original triangle.

## Description

In right ΔABC with right angle at B and BD ⊥ AC (D on AC): ΔABD ~ ΔABC ~ ΔCBD (with careful vertex order). Corresponding sides then give geometric-mean relations such as AB² = AD · AC, CB² = CD · CA, and BD² = AD · DC.

This similarity is the usual route to proving the [[Pythagoras Theorem]].
