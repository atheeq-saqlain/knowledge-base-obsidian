# Basic Proportionality Theorem

## Definition

If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle intersecting the other two sides in distinct points, then those other two sides are divided in the same ratio (Thales’ theorem). Conversely, if a line divides two sides of a triangle in the same ratio, it is parallel to the third side.

## Description

In ΔABC with DE ∥ BC (D on AB, E on AC), AD/DB = AE/EC.

Applications: checking whether EF ∥ QR from segment lengths; proving mid-point results; ratios on trapezium diagonals.
