# Circle

## Definition

A circle is the set of all points in a plane at a fixed distance (the radius) from a fixed point (the centre).

## Description

A chord is a line segment joining two points on the circle; a diameter is a chord through the centre. Relative to a straight line in the same plane, the circle may share no points (non-intersecting line), exactly two points (secant), or exactly one point (tangent).
