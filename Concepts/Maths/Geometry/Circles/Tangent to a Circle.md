# Tangent to a Circle

## Definition

A tangent to a [[Circle]] is a line that intersects the circle in exactly one point, called the point of contact. A line meeting the circle in two points is a [[Secant to a Circle]].

## Description

From a point inside the circle there is no tangent; at a point on the circle there is exactly one; from an external point there are exactly two. A circle has infinitely many tangents (one at each point) and at most two parallel tangents. The word tangent comes from Latin tangere, “to touch.” The line containing the radius through the point of contact is the normal to the circle at that point.
