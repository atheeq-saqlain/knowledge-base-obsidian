# Tangent to a Circle

## Definition

A tangent to a circle is a line that intersects the circle in exactly one point (the point of contact). A line meeting the circle in two points is a secant.

## Description

From a point inside the circle: no tangent. On the circle: exactly one tangent. Outside: exactly two tangents. A circle can have infinitely many tangents (one at each point) and at most two parallel tangents.
