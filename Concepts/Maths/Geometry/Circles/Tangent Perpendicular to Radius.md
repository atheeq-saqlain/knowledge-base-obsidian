# Tangent Perpendicular to Radius

## Definition

The [[Tangent to a Circle]] at any point is perpendicular to the radius through the point of contact.

## Description

If O is the centre and P the point of contact of tangent PQ, then OP ⊥ PQ. Combined with Pythagoras this finds tangent lengths or radii (e.g. OQ hypotenuse, OP radius, PQ tangent).
