# Sector of a Circle

## Definition

A sector is the portion of a circular disk enclosed by two radii and the corresponding arc.

## Description

The central angle θ between the radii is the angle of the sector. A minor sector has θ < 180° (usually); the major sector is the rest of the disk with angle 360° − θ. Unless stated otherwise, “sector” means the minor sector. A quadrant is a sector with θ = 90°.
