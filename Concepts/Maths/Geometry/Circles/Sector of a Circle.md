# Sector of a Circle

## Definition

A sector is the region enclosed by two radii and the corresponding arc; the angle between the radii is the angle of the sector.

## Description

Minor sector corresponds to the smaller central angle; major sector to 360° minus that angle. Unless stated otherwise, “sector” means the minor sector.
