# Secant to a Circle

## Definition

A secant to a [[Circle]] is a line that intersects the circle in exactly two distinct points.

## Description

The segment joining those two points is a chord of the circle. A tangent is the limiting case of a secant when the two intersection points coincide. At most two tangents can be drawn parallel to a given secant.
