# AA Similarity Criterion

## Definition

If two angles of one triangle are respectively equal to two angles of another triangle, then the triangles are similar (AA). Equivalently, if all three corresponding angles are equal (AAA), the triangles are similar and corresponding sides are proportional.

## Description

Because the third angle is determined by the angle sum, two equal angles suffice. Write the similarity with correct vertex order, e.g. ΔABC ~ ΔPQR when ∠A = ∠P and ∠B = ∠Q.

Often used with parallel lines (corresponding/alternate angles) or shared angles.
