# Trigonometric Ratios of Standard Angles

## Definition

Exact values of the trigonometric ratios at 0°, 30°, 45°, 60° and 90° are fixed by geometry (isosceles right triangles and half-equilateral triangles). In particular sin 0° = 0, sin 30° = 1/2, sin 45° = 1/√2, sin 60° = √3/2, sin 90° = 1, with cosine the reverse sequence, and tan, csc, sec, cot obtained from these (some undefined at 0° or 90°).

## Description

As an acute angle increases from 0° to 90°, sine increases from 0 to 1 and cosine decreases from 1 to 0. These values are used to evaluate numerical expressions and to solve for unknown angles when a ratio matches a known standard value.
