# Trigonometric Ratios

## Definition

In a right triangle ABC right-angled at B, with acute angle θ = ∠A: hypotenuse is AC, opposite side is BC, and adjacent side is AB. The six ratios are sin θ = BC/AC, cos θ = AB/AC, tan θ = BC/AB, csc θ = 1/sin θ, sec θ = 1/cos θ, and cot θ = 1/tan θ.

## Description

Also tan θ = sin θ / cos θ and cot θ = cos θ / sin θ. The ratios depend only on the angle, not on the size of the triangle. Because the hypotenuse is longest, 0 ≤ sin θ ≤ 1 and 0 ≤ cos θ ≤ 1 for acute θ, while sec θ ≥ 1 and csc θ ≥ 1 when defined.
