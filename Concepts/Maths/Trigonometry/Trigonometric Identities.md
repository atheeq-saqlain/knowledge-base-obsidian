# Trigonometric Identities

## Definition

The fundamental identities (for angles where the expressions are defined) are sin² A + cos² A = 1, 1 + tan² A = sec² A, and 1 + cot² A = csc² A.

## Description

They follow from Pythagoras on a right triangle (or the unit circle). Most textbook proofs rewrite both sides in sin and cos, then apply one of these three. Related quotient forms tan A = sin A / cos A and cot A = cos A / sin A are used constantly.
