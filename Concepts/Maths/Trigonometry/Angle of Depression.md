# Angle of Depression

## Definition

The angle of depression of an object is the angle between the [[Line of Sight]] and the horizontal when the object lies **below** the horizontal level (the observer looks down).

## Description

By alternate interior angles with a horizontal, the angle of depression from a high point equals the angle of elevation from the lower point to that high point. Use this to place the known angle inside the right triangle on the ground or at the base of a tower.
