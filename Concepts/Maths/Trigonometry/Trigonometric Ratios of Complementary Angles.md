# Trigonometric Ratios of Complementary Angles

## Definition

If A + B = 90°, then sin(90° − A) = cos A, cos(90° − A) = sin A, tan(90° − A) = cot A, cot(90° − A) = tan A, sec(90° − A) = csc A, and csc(90° − A) = sec A.

## Description

In a right triangle the two acute angles are complementary, so each ratio of one equals the co-ratio of the other. Use these to rewrite angles greater than 45° in terms of angles between 0° and 45°, and to simplify products such as tan θ tan(90° − θ) = 1.
