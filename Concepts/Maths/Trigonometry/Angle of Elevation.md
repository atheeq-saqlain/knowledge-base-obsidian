# Angle of Elevation

## Definition

The angle of elevation of an object is the angle between the [[Line of Sight]] and the horizontal when the object lies **above** the horizontal level (the observer looks up).

## Description

In the usual right triangle, this angle sits at the observer’s eye; opposite is the vertical rise to the object and adjacent is the horizontal distance. Solve with trigonometric ratios of standard angles (e.g. tan θ = height/distance).
