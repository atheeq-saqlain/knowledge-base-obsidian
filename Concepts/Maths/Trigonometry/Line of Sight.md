# Line of Sight

## Definition

The line of sight is the straight line from an observer’s eye to a point on the object being viewed.

## Description

Heights-and-distances problems model this line as the hypotenuse (or another side) of a right triangle formed with a horizontal through the eye and a vertical through the object. Angles between the line of sight and the horizontal are the [[Angle of Elevation]] or [[Angle of Depression]].
