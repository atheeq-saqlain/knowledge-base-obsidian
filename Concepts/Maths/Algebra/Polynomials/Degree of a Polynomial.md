# Degree of a Polynomial

## Definition

If p(x) is a polynomial in x, the **degree** of p(x) is the highest power of x that appears with a non-zero coefficient. Expressions such as 1/(x−1), √x + 2, and 1/(x² + 2x + 3) are not polynomials.

## Description

Classification by degree: degree 1 is linear (e.g. 2x − 3); degree 2 is quadratic, general form ax² + bx + c with a ≠ 0; degree 3 is cubic, general form ax³ + bx² + cx + d with a ≠ 0. A polynomial of degree n has at most n real zeroes.
