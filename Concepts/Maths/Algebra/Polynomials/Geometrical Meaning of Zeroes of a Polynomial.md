# Geometrical Meaning of Zeroes of a Polynomial

## Definition

The zeroes of a polynomial p(x) are precisely the x-coordinates of the points where the graph of y = p(x) intersects the x-axis.

## Description

A linear polynomial graphs as a straight line and meets the x-axis at exactly one point. A quadratic graphs as a parabola (opens up if a > 0, down if a < 0) and may meet the x-axis at two distinct points, one coincident point (touch), or not at all. A cubic can meet the x-axis at most three times. Counting intersection points gives the number of real zeroes ([[Zero of a Polynomial]]).
