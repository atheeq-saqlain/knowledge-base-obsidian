# Zeroes and Coefficients of a Cubic

## Definition

If α, β, γ are the zeroes of ax³ + bx² + cx + d (a ≠ 0), then α + β + γ = −b/a, αβ + βγ + γα = c/a, and αβγ = −d/a. A cubic with these elementary symmetric sums S₁, S₂, S₃ has the form k(x³ − S₁x² + S₂x − S₃).

## Description

Used to verify given zeroes of a cubic and to build a cubic from prescribed sum, sum of products two at a time, and product. Analogous to the quadratic case ([[Zeroes and Coefficients of a Quadratic]]).
