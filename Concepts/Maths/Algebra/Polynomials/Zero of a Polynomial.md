# Zero of a Polynomial

## Definition

If p(x) is a polynomial and k is a real number, the value of the polynomial at k is p(k). A real number k is a **zero** of p(x) if p(k) = 0. For the linear polynomial ax + b (a ≠ 0), the unique zero is k = −b/a.

## Description

Finding zeroes means solving p(x) = 0. Geometrically, zeroes are the x-coordinates where the graph of y = p(x) meets the x-axis ([[Geometrical Meaning of Zeroes of a Polynomial]]). For quadratics and cubics, zeroes are also linked to coefficients by sum and product identities.
