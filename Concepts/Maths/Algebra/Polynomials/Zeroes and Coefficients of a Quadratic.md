# Zeroes and Coefficients of a Quadratic

## Definition

If α and β are the zeroes of ax² + bx + c (a ≠ 0), then α + β = −b/a and αβ = c/a. A quadratic with given sum S and product P of zeroes has the form k(x² − Sx + P) for a non-zero constant k.

## Description

Verify by finding zeroes (factorisation or formula) and checking sum and product against −b/a and c/a. To construct a polynomial from sum and product, take k = 1 (or clear denominators with a suitable k). Related to [[Zero of a Polynomial]] and [[Degree of a Polynomial]].
