# Substitution Method

## Definition

Solve a [[Pair of Linear Equations]] by expressing one variable from one equation in terms of the other, substituting into the second equation, then back-substituting to find the remaining variable.

## Description

Steps: (1) solve one equation for one variable; (2) substitute into the other; (3) solve the resulting one-variable equation; (4) substitute back. A true statement with no variable means infinitely many solutions; a false statement means inconsistent.
