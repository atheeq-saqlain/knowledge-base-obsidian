# Quadratic Formula

## Definition

The roots of ax² + bx + c = 0 (a ≠ 0) are x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac))/(2a), provided b² − 4ac ≥ 0 for real roots. The formula is obtained by [[Completing the Square]].

## Description

Use when factorisation is awkward. The expression under the square root is the [[Discriminant of a Quadratic]]; its sign decides whether real roots exist and whether they are distinct or equal.
