# Arithmetic Mean

## Definition

If three numbers a, b, c are in an [[Arithmetic Progression]], then b = (a + c)/2; this middle value b is the arithmetic mean of a and c.

## Description

Each interior term of an AP is the arithmetic mean of its two neighbours. To fill missing terms between two known terms, the common difference is (last − first) divided by the number of gaps.

Example: for 2, □, 26 the missing term is (2 + 26)/2 = 14. For −4, □, □, □, □, 6 there are 5 gaps, so d = (6 − (−4))/5 = 2 and the completed AP is −4, −2, 0, 2, 4, 6.
