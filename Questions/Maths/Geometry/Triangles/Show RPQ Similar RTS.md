# Show RPQ Similar RTS

## Statement

S and T are points on sides PR and QR of ΔPQR such that ∠P = ∠RTS. Show that ΔRPQ ~ ΔRTS.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.3, Question 5.

## Correct Answer

∠P = ∠RTS (given) and ∠PRQ = ∠TRS (common). By AA, ΔRPQ ~ ΔRTS.
