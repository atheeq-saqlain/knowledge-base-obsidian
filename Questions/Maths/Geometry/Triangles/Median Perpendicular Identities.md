# Median Perpendicular Identities

## Statement

In Fig. 2.60, AD is a median of a triangle ABC and AM ⊥ BC. Prove that:

(i) AC² = AD² + BC · DM + (BC/2)²
(ii) AB² = AD² − BC · DM + (BC/2)²
(iii) AC² + AB² = 2 AD² + (1/2) BC²

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.6, Question 5.

## Correct Answer

Apply Pythagoras in the right triangles formed by AM; use D mid-point of BC and relate DM. Part (iii) follows by adding (i) and (ii).
