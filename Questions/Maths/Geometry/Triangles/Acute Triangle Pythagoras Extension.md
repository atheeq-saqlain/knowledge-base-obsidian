## Statement

In Fig. 2.59, ABC is a triangle in which ∠ABC < 90° and AD ⊥ BC. Prove that AC² = AB² + BC² − 2 BC · BD.

## Description

## Correct Answer

Apply Pythagoras in ΔADB and ΔADC; eliminate AD² to get AC² = AB² + BC² − 2 BC · BD.
