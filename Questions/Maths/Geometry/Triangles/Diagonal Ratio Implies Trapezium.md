# Diagonal Ratio Implies Trapezium

## Statement

The diagonals of a quadrilateral ABCD intersect each other at the point O such that AO/BO = CO/DO. Show that ABCD is a trapezium.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.2, Question 10.

## Correct Answer

From AO/BO = CO/DO we get AO/CO = BO/DO. With vertical angles at O, ΔAOB ~ ΔCOD (SAS similarity of sides about the included vertical angle, or SSS of ratios). Corresponding angles equal ⇒ AB ∥ DC, so ABCD is a trapezium.
