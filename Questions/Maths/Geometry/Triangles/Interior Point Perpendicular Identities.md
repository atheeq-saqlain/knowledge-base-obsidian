# Interior Point Perpendicular Identities

## Statement

In Fig. 2.54, O is a point in the interior of a triangle ABC, OD ⊥ BC, OE ⊥ AC and OF ⊥ AB. Show that:

(i) OA² + OB² + OC² − OD² − OE² − OF² = AF² + BD² + CE²
(ii) AF² + BD² + CE² = AE² + CD² + BF²

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.5, Question 8.

## Correct Answer

Apply Pythagoras in the six right triangles formed by the perpendiculars from O; rearrange to obtain both identities.
