# Parallelogram Produced Side Similarity

## Statement

E is a point on the side AD produced of a parallelogram ABCD and BE intersects CD at F. Show that ΔABE ~ ΔCFB.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.3, Question 8.

## Correct Answer

In a parallelogram, AB ∥ DC and AD ∥ BC. Alternate/corresponding angles give ∠BAE = ∠BCF and ∠AEB = ∠CBF (or equivalent). By AA, ΔABE ~ ΔCFB.
