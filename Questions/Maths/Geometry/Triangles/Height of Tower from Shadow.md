# Height of Tower from Shadow

## Statement

A vertical pole of length 6 m casts a shadow 4 m long on the ground and at the same time a tower casts a shadow 28 m long. Find the height of the tower.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.3, Question 15.

## Correct Answer

42 m

At the same time, sun rays make equal angles, so the right triangles are similar (AA).
Height/shadow ratios: h/28 = 6/4 ⇒ h = 28 × (6/4) = 42 m.
