# Proportional Sides Median Similarity

## Statement

Sides AB and BC and median AD of a triangle ABC are respectively proportional to sides PQ and QR and median PM of ΔPQR. Show that ΔABC ~ ΔPQR.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.3, Question 12.

## Correct Answer

AB/PQ = BC/QR = AD/PM. Construct or use the property that medians create proportional half-bases; SSS or SAS similarity of the half-triangles, then the wholes, yields ΔABC ~ ΔPQR.
