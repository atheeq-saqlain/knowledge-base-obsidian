# Right Triangle Scale Factor Five Thirds

## Statement

Draw a right triangle with legs 4 cm and 3 cm. Construct another triangle whose sides are 5/3 times the corresponding sides of the given triangle. Provide mathematical justification.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 6.1, Question 7.

## Correct Answer

Build the right triangle (legs 3 cm, 4 cm), then apply Construction 6.2 with scale 5/3. Justify with BPT / AA similarity from the constructed parallels.
