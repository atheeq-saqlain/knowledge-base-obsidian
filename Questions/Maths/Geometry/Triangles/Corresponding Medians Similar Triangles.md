# Corresponding Medians Similar Triangles

## Statement

If AD and PM are medians of triangles ABC and PQR, respectively, where ΔABC ~ ΔPQR, prove that AB/PQ = AD/PM.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.3, Question 16.

## Correct Answer

Similarity gives AB/PQ = BC/QR = CA/RP. Medians to corresponding sides are proportional to the sides, so AB/PQ = AD/PM.
