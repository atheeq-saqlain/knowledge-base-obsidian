# Obtuse Triangle Pythagoras Extension

## Statement

In Fig. 2.58, ABC is a triangle in which ∠ABC > 90° and AD ⊥ CB produced. Prove that AC² = AB² + BC² + 2 BC · BD.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.6, Question 3.

## Correct Answer

Apply Pythagoras in ΔADB and ΔADC (or ΔADC with BD on the extension) and rearrange to AC² = AB² + BC² + 2 BC · BD.
