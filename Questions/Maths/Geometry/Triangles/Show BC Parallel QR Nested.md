# Show BC Parallel QR Nested

## Statement

In Fig. 2.21, A, B and C are points on OP, OQ and OR respectively such that AB ∥ PQ and AC ∥ PR. Show that BC ∥ QR.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.2, Question 6.

## Correct Answer

BPT gives OA/AP = OB/BQ and OA/AP = OC/CR. Hence OB/BQ = OC/CR. Converse of BPT in ΔOQR ⇒ BC ∥ QR.
