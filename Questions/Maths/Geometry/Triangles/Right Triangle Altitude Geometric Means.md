# Right Triangle Altitude Geometric Means

## Statement

In Fig. 2.53, ABD is a triangle right-angled at A and AC ⊥ BD. Show that:

(i) AB² = BC · BD
(ii) AC² = BC · DC
(iii) AD² = BD · CD

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.5, Question 3.

## Correct Answer

Each follows from [[Right Triangle Altitude Similarity]]: a leg is the geometric mean of the hypotenuse and the projection adjacent to that leg; the altitude is the geometric mean of the two segments.
