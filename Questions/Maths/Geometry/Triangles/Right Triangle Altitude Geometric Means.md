## Statement

(i) AB² = BC · BD

## Description

## Correct Answer

Each follows from [[Right Triangle Altitude Similarity]]: a leg is the geometric mean of the hypotenuse and the projection adjacent to that leg; the altitude is the geometric mean of the two segments.
