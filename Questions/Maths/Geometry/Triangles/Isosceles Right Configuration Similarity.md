# Isosceles Right Configuration Similarity

## Statement

In Fig. 2.40, E is a point on side CB produced of an isosceles triangle ABC with AB = AC. If AD ⊥ BC and EF ⊥ AC, prove that ΔABD ~ ΔECF.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.3, Question 11.

## Correct Answer

AB = AC ⇒ base angles equal. Right angles at D and F plus equal angles from the isosceles / figure give AA ⇒ ΔABD ~ ΔECF.
