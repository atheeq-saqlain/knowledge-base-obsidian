# Angle Bisectors Similar Triangles

## Statement

CD and GH are respectively the bisectors of ∠ACB and ∠EGF such that D and H lie on sides AB and FE of ΔABC and ΔEFG respectively. If ΔABC ~ ΔFEG, show that:

(i) CD/GH = AC/FG
(ii) ΔDCB ~ ΔHGE
(iii) ΔDCA ~ ΔHGF

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.3, Question 10.

## Correct Answer

Corresponding angles of similar triangles are equal; bisectors create equal half-angles. Apply AA for (ii)–(iii) and corresponding sides / angle-bisector proportionality for (i).
