# Show EF Parallel QR Nested

## Statement

In Fig. 2.20, DE ∥ OQ and DF ∥ OR. Show that EF ∥ QR.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.2, Question 5.

## Correct Answer

By BPT in ΔPOQ and ΔPOR, PE/EQ = PD/DO and PF/FR = PD/DO. Thus PE/EQ = PF/FR. By the converse of BPT in ΔPQR, EF ∥ QR.
