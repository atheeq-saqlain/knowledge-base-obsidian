# Midpoints Join Parallel Third Side

## Statement

Using Theorem 2.2 (converse of BPT), prove that the line joining the mid-points of any two sides of a triangle is parallel to the third side.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.2, Question 8.

## Correct Answer

Let D, E be mid-points of AB, AC. Then AD/DB = AE/EC = 1. By the converse of BPT, DE ∥ BC.
