# Similar Triangle Scale Factor Two Thirds

## Statement

Construct a triangle of sides 4 cm, 5 cm and 6 cm and then a triangle similar to it whose sides are 2/3 of the corresponding sides of the first triangle.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 6.1, Question 2.

## Correct Answer

Build ΔABC with sides 4, 5, 6 cm. Scale factor 2/3 gives sides ≈ 2.67 cm, 3.33 cm, 4 cm via [[Similar Triangle Construction]] (Construction 6.2).
