# Altitudes Intersect Similarity

## Statement

In Fig. 2.38, altitudes AD and CE of ΔABC intersect each other at the point P. Show that:

(i) ΔAEP ~ ΔCDP
(ii) ΔABD ~ ΔCBE
(iii) ΔAEP ~ ΔABD
(iv) ΔPDC ~ ΔBEC

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.3, Question 7.

## Correct Answer

Each pair shares right angles at the feet of the altitudes and a common or vertical angle; apply AA repeatedly to obtain the four similarities.
