# Perpendicular DB Three CD Identity

## Statement

The perpendicular from A on side BC of a ΔABC intersects BC at D such that DB = 3 CD. Prove that 2 AB² = 2 AC² + BC².

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.5, Question 14.

## Correct Answer

Let CD = x, DB = 3x, BC = 4x. Apply Pythagoras in ΔABD and ΔACD and eliminate AD² to obtain 2 AB² = 2 AC² + BC².
