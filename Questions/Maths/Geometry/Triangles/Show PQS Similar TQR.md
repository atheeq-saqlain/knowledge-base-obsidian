# Show PQS Similar TQR

## Statement

In Fig. 2.36, QR/QS = QT/PR and ∠1 = ∠2. Show that ΔPQS ~ ΔTQR.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.3, Question 4.

## Correct Answer

∠1 = ∠2 ⇒ the triangles share equal angles as labelled; with the given side proportion involving the included/adjacent configuration, apply SAS or AA as in the textbook figure to conclude ΔPQS ~ ΔTQR.
