# Similar Triangle Scale Factor Four Thirds

## Statement

Draw ΔABC with BC = 7 cm, ∠B = 45°, ∠A = 105°. Construct a triangle whose sides are 4/3 times the corresponding sides of ΔABC. Provide mathematical justification.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 6.1, Question 6.

## Correct Answer

Note ∠C = 30°. Apply Construction 6.2 with scale factor 4/3 (> 1): extend sides and draw parallels through the 4th of 3 equal steps. Justify with BPT / AA similarity.
