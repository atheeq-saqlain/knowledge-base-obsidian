# Areas Ratio Square of Medians

## Statement

Prove that the ratio of the areas of two similar triangles is equal to the square of the ratio of their corresponding medians.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.4, Question 6.

## Correct Answer

Corresponding medians of similar triangles are proportional to corresponding sides. Area ratio = (side ratio)² = (median ratio)².
