# Angle B in Triangle Sides 6 Root 3

## Statement

In ΔABC, AB = 6√3 cm, AC = 12 cm and BC = 6 cm. The angle B is:

(A) 120°  (B) 60°  (C) 90°  (D) 45°.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.5, Question 17.

## Correct Answer

(C) 90° — 6² + (6√3)² = 36 + 108 = 144 = 12², so by converse of Pythagoras ∠B = 90°.
