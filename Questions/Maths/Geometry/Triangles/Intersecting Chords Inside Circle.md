# Intersecting Chords Inside Circle

## Statement

In Fig. 2.61, two chords AB and CD intersect each other at the point P. Prove that:

(i) ΔAPC ~ ΔDPB
(ii) AP · PB = CP · DP

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.6, Question 7.

## Correct Answer

Vertical angles at P and angles in the same segment give AA ⇒ ΔAPC ~ ΔDPB. Corresponding sides: AP/DP = CP/BP ⇒ AP · PB = CP · DP.
