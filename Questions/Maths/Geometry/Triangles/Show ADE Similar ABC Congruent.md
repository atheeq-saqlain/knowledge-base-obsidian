## Correct Answer

Congruence gives AB = AC, BE = CD, ∠BAE = ∠CAD, etc. Then ∠DAE = ∠BAC (common or sum of equal angles) and AD/AB = AE/AC from the congruent correspondence; AA or SAS similarity yields ΔADE ~ ΔABC.
