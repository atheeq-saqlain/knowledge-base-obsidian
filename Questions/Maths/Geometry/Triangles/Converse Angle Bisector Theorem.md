# Converse Angle Bisector Theorem

## Statement

In Fig. 2.63, D is a point on side BC of ΔABC such that BD/CD = AB/AC. Prove that AD is the bisector of ∠BAC.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.6, Question 9.

## Correct Answer

Converse of the angle-bisector theorem: construct or use similarity/area to show ∠BAD = ∠CAD.
