# Medial Triangle Area Ratio

## Statement

D, E and F are respectively the mid-points of sides AB, BC and CA of ΔABC. Find the ratio of the areas of ΔDEF and ΔABC.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.4, Question 5.

## Correct Answer

The medial triangle DEF has sides parallel to ABC and half as long (mid-point theorem). Side ratio 1 : 2 ⇒ area ratio 1 : 4.
