# Two Planes Distance After Flight

## Statement

An aeroplane leaves an airport and flies due north at a speed of 1000 km/h. At the same time, another aeroplane leaves the same airport and flies due west at a speed of 1200 km/h. How far apart will the two planes be after 1½ hours?

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.5, Question 11.

## Correct Answer

Distances 1500 km north and 1800 km west; separation √(1500² + 1800²) = 300√61 km.
