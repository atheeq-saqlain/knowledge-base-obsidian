# Parallelogram Diagonals Squares

## Statement

Prove that the sum of the squares of the diagonals of a parallelogram is equal to the sum of the squares of its sides.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.6, Question 6.

## Correct Answer

Use vector identity or apply Pythagoras on the two triangles formed by one diagonal (twice) to get p² + q² = 2(a² + b²).
