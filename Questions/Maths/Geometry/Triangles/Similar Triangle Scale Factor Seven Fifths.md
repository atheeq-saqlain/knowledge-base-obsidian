# Similar Triangle Scale Factor Seven Fifths

## Statement

Construct a triangle with sides 5 cm, 6 cm and 7 cm and then another triangle whose sides are 7/5 of the corresponding sides of the first triangle. Provide mathematical justification.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 6.1, Question 3.

## Correct Answer

Follow Construction 6.2 with scale factor 7/5 (> 1): extend BC and BA, mark 7 equal steps on a ray from B, join the 5th point to C, and draw a parallel through the 7th. Justify with BPT / AA similarity.
