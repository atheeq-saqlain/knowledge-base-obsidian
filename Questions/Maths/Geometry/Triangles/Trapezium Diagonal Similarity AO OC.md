# Trapezium Diagonal Similarity AO OC

## Statement

Diagonals AC and BD of a trapezium ABCD with AB ∥ DC intersect each other at the point O. Using a similarity criterion for two triangles, show that AO/OC = OB/OD.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.3, Question 3.

## Correct Answer

AB ∥ DC ⇒ alternate/corresponding angles equal ⇒ ΔAOB ~ ΔCOD (AA) ⇒ AO/CO = BO/DO.
