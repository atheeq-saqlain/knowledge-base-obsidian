# Trapezium Diagonal Ratio AO BO

## Statement

ABCD is a trapezium in which AB ∥ DC and its diagonals intersect each other at the point O. Show that AO/BO = CO/DO.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.2, Question 9.

## Correct Answer

AB ∥ DC ⇒ ΔAOB ~ ΔCOD by AA (alternate/corresponding angles). Corresponding sides give AO/CO = BO/DO, which rearranges to AO/BO = CO/DO.
