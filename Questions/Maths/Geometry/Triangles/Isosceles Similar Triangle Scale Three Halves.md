# Isosceles Similar Triangle Scale Three Halves

## Statement

Construct an isosceles triangle with base 8 cm and altitude 4 cm, then another triangle whose sides are 3/2 times the corresponding sides of the isosceles triangle. Provide mathematical justification.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 6.1, Question 4.

## Correct Answer

Build the isosceles triangle (base 8 cm, altitude 4 cm), then apply Construction 6.2 with scale 3/2. Justify with BPT / AA similarity from the constructed parallels.
