# Isosceles AB Squared Implies Right

## Statement

ABC is an isosceles triangle with AC = BC. If AB² = 2 AC², prove that ABC is a right triangle.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.5, Question 5.

## Correct Answer

AB² = 2 AC² = AC² + BC² (since AC = BC). By the converse of Pythagoras, ∠C = 90°.
