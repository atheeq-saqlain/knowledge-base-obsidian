# Altitude Feet Geometric Means

## Statement

In Fig. 2.57, D is a point on hypotenuse AC of ΔABC, such that BD ⊥ AC, DM ⊥ BC and DN ⊥ AB. Prove that:

(i) DM² = DN · MC
(ii) DN² = DM · AN

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.6, Question 2.

## Correct Answer

Right-triangle altitude / geometric-mean relations in the small right triangles formed by the perpendiculars.
