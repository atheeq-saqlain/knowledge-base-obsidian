# CA Squared Equals CB Times CD

## Statement

D is a point on the side BC of a triangle ABC such that ∠ADC = ∠BAC. Show that CA² = CB · CD.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.3, Question 13.

## Correct Answer

∠ADC = ∠BAC and ∠ACD common ⇒ ΔADC ~ ΔBAC (AA). Corresponding sides: CA/CB = CD/CA ⇒ CA² = CB · CD.
