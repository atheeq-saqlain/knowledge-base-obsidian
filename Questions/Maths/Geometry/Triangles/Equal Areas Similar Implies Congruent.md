# Equal Areas Similar Implies Congruent

## Statement

If the areas of two similar triangles are equal, prove that they are congruent.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.4, Question 4.

## Correct Answer

Area ratio 1 ⇒ side ratio 1 (since area ratio = (side ratio)²). Corresponding sides equal ⇒ the triangles are congruent.
