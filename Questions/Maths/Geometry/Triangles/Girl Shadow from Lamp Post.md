# Girl Shadow from Lamp Post

## Statement

A girl of height 90 cm is walking away from the base of a lamp-post at a speed of 1.2 m/s. If the lamp is 3.6 m above the ground, find the length of her shadow after 4 seconds.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Example 3 (Chapter 2).

## Correct Answer

1.6 m

Distance walked BD = 1.2 × 4 = 4.8 m. Girl height CD = 0.9 m; lamp AB = 3.6 m; shadow DE = x.
ΔABE ~ ΔCDE by AA (right angles at ground and common angle at E).
(4.8 + x)/x = 3.6/0.9 = 4 ⇒ 4.8 + x = 4x ⇒ x = 1.6 m.
