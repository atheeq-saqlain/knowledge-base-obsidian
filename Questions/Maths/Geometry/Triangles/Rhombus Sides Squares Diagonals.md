# Rhombus Sides Squares Diagonals

## Statement

Prove that the sum of the squares of the sides of a rhombus is equal to the sum of the squares of its diagonals.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.5, Question 7.

## Correct Answer

Diagonals of a rhombus are perpendicular bisectors. In each of the four right triangles, apply Pythagoras; summing gives 4a² = p² + q² (equivalently sum of squares of sides = sum of squares of diagonals).
