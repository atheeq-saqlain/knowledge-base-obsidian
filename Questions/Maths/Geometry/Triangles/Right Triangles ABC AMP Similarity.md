# Right Triangles ABC AMP Similarity

## Statement

(i) ΔABC ~ ΔAMP

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.3, Question 9.

## Correct Answer

(i) ∠A common; right angles at B and M ⇒ AA ⇒ ΔABC ~ ΔAMP.
(ii) Corresponding sides: CA/PA = BC/MP.
