# Find EC and AD with DE Parallel BC

## Statement

In ΔABC, DE ∥ BC with D on AB and E on AC. Find EC in (i) and AD in (ii).

(i) AD = 1.5 cm, DB = 3 cm, AE = 1 cm

(ii) DB = 7.2 cm, AE = 1.8 cm, EC = 5.4 cm

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.2, Question 1.

## Correct Answer

(i) By BPT, AD/DB = AE/EC ⇒ 1.5/3 = 1/EC ⇒ EC = 2 cm.

(ii) AD/DB = AE/EC ⇒ AD/7.2 = 1.8/5.4 ⇒ AD/7.2 = 1/3 ⇒ AD = 2.4 cm.
