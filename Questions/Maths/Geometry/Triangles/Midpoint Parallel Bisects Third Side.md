# Midpoint Parallel Bisects Third Side

## Statement

Using Theorem 2.1 (BPT), prove that a line drawn through the mid-point of one side of a triangle parallel to another side bisects the third side.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.2, Question 7.

## Correct Answer

Let D be the mid-point of AB and DE ∥ BC with E on AC. Then AD/DB = 1. By BPT, AE/EC = AD/DB = 1, so E is the mid-point of AC.
