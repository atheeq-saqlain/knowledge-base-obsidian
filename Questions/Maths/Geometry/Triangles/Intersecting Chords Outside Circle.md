# Intersecting Chords Outside Circle

## Statement

In Fig. 2.62, two chords AB and CD of a circle intersect each other at the point P (when produced) outside the circle. Prove that:

(i) ΔPAC ~ ΔPDB
(ii) PA · PB = PC · PD

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.6, Question 8.

## Correct Answer

Shared angle at P and angles in the same segment give AA ⇒ ΔPAC ~ ΔPDB. Corresponding sides yield PA · PB = PC · PD.
