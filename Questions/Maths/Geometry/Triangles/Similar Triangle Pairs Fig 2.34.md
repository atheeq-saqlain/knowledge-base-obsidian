## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.3, Question 1.

## Correct Answer

(i) ∠A = ∠P = 60°, ∠B = ∠Q = 80°, ∠C = ∠R = 40° ⇒ ΔABC ~ ΔPQR (AAA).

(ii) AB/QR = BC/RP = CA/PQ = 1/2 ⇒ ΔABC ~ ΔQRP (SSS).

(iii) LM/EF ≠ MP/DE = LP/DF, so ΔLMP and ΔDEF are not similar.

(iv) MN/QP = ML/QR and ∠M = ∠Q = 70° ⇒ ΔMNL ~ ΔQPR (SAS).

(v) The equal 80° angles are not the included angles of the proportional sides, so ΔABC and ΔDEF are not similar.

(vi) ∠F = 30° and ∠P = 70° ⇒ ΔDEF ~ ΔPQR (AAA).
