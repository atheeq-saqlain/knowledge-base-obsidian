# Prove AM AB Equals AN AD

## Statement

In Fig. 2.18, if LM ∥ CB and LN ∥ CD, prove that AM/AB = AN/AD.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.2, Question 3.

## Correct Answer

Apply BPT in ΔABC (LM ∥ CB) and in ΔACD (LN ∥ CD). Then AM/AB = AL/AC and AN/AD = AL/AC, so AM/AB = AN/AD.
