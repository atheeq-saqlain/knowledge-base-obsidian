# Similar Triangle Scale Factor Three Fourths

## Statement

Draw ΔABC with BC = 6 cm, AB = 5 cm, ∠ABC = 60°. Construct a triangle whose sides are 3/4 of the corresponding sides of ΔABC. Provide mathematical justification.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 6.1, Question 5.

## Correct Answer

Apply Construction 6.2 with scale factor 3/4 (< 1): mark 4 equal steps on a ray from B, join the 4th point to C, and draw a parallel through the 3rd. Justify with BPT / AA similarity.
