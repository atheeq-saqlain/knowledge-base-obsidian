# Tangents from Centres of Two Circles

## Statement

Draw AB = 8 cm. With centre A draw a circle of radius 4 cm; with centre B draw a circle of radius 3 cm. Construct tangents to each circle from the centre of the other. Provide mathematical justification.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 6.2, Question 5.

## Correct Answer

Tangents from B to circle A: length √(8² − 4²) = √48 = 4√3 cm. Tangents from A to circle B: √(8² − 3²) = √55 cm. Use Construction 6.3 from each centre to the other circle.
