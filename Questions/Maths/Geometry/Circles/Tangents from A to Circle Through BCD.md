# Tangents from A to Circle Through BCD

## Statement

Right ΔABC with AB = 6 cm, BC = 8 cm, ∠B = 90°; BD ⊥ AC. Draw the circle through B, C, D and construct the tangents from A to this circle. Provide mathematical justification.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 6.2, Question 6.

## Correct Answer

Circle through B, C, D has diameter BC (∠BDC = 90°, angle in a semicircle). Tangents from A: use Construction 6.3 with that circle’s centre (mid-point of BC).
