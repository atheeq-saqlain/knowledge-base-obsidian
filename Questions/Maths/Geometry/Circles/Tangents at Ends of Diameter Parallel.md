# Tangents at Ends of Diameter Parallel

## Statement

Prove that the tangents drawn at the ends of a diameter of a circle are parallel.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 4.2, Question 4.

## Correct Answer

Tangents at ends A, B of diameter AB are each perpendicular to the diameter (radii OA, OB along the diameter), so both are ⊥ AB. Therefore the tangents are parallel.
