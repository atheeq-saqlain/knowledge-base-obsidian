# Tangents Supplementary Central Angle

## Statement

Prove that the angle between the two tangents drawn from an external point to a circle is supplementary to the angle subtended by the line-segment joining the points of contact at the centre.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 4.2, Question 10.

## Correct Answer

In the quadrilateral formed by two radii and two tangents, two right angles leave ∠ at the external point + ∠ at the centre = 180°.
