# Tangents from Points on Extended Diameter

## Statement

Draw a circle of radius 3 cm. Take points P and Q on one extended diameter, each 7 cm from the centre. Draw tangents to the circle from P and from Q. Provide mathematical justification.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 6.2, Question 3.

## Correct Answer

From each of P and Q use Construction 6.3 (helper circle on the segment from the point to O). Each tangent length = √(7² − 3²) = √40 = 2√10 cm.
