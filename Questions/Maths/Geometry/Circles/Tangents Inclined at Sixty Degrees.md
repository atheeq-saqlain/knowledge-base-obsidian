# Tangents Inclined at Sixty Degrees

## Statement

Draw a pair of tangents to a circle of radius 5 cm that are inclined to each other at 60°. Provide mathematical justification.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 6.2, Question 4.

## Correct Answer

Construct ∠TPQ = 60° at an external point T with TP = TQ; equivalently build the two tangents so that the central angle between contact points is 120° (supplementary to 60°), then draw the tangents.
