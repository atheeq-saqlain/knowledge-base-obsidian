# Tangents to Freehand Bangle Circle

## Statement

Draw a circle with the help of a bangle. Take a point outside the circle and construct the pair of tangents from that point. Provide mathematical justification.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 6.2, Question 7.

## Correct Answer

Locate the centre as the intersection of perpendicular bisectors of two chords of the freehand circle, then apply Construction 6.3 from the external point.
