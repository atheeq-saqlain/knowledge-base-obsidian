# Circumscribed Triangle Sides AB AC

## Statement

A triangle ABC is drawn to circumscribe a circle of radius 4 cm such that the segments BD and DC into which BC is divided by the point of contact D are of lengths 8 cm and 6 cm respectively. Find the lengths of the sides AB and AC.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 4.2, Question 12.

## Correct Answer

Let equal tangent segments from A, B, C be x, y, z with y = BD = 8, z = DC = 6. Using r = A/s (area/semiperimeter) gives x = 7; AB = x + 8 = 15 cm, AC = x + 6 = 13 cm.
