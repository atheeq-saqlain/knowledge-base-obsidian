# Opposite Sides Supplementary at Centre

## Statement

Prove that opposite sides of a quadrilateral circumscribing a circle subtend supplementary angles at the centre of the circle.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 4.2, Question 13.

## Correct Answer

Join the centre to the vertices / points of contact; use equal radii and equal tangent lengths to show opposite central angles sum to 180°.
