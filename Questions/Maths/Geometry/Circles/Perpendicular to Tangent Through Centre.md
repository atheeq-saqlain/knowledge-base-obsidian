# Perpendicular to Tangent Through Centre

## Statement

Prove that the perpendicular at the point of contact to the tangent to a circle passes through the centre.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 4.2, Question 5.

## Correct Answer

The radius through the point of contact is already ⊥ to the tangent. The unique line through the contact point perpendicular to the tangent is that radius line, which contains the centre.
