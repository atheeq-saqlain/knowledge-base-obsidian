# Hanif Coin Toss Three Times Lose

## Statement

A game consists of tossing a one rupee coin 3 times and noting its outcome each time. Hanif wins if all the tosses give the same result (three heads or three tails), and loses otherwise. Calculate the probability that Hanif will lose the game.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 14.1, Question 23.

## Correct Answer

3/4
