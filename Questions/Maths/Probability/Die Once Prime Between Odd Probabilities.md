# Die Once Prime Between Odd Probabilities

## Statement

A die is thrown once. Find the probability of getting:

(i) a prime number;

(ii) a number lying between 2 and 6;

(iii) an odd number.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 14.1, Question 13.

## Correct Answer

(i) 1/2
(ii) 1/2
(iii) 1/2
