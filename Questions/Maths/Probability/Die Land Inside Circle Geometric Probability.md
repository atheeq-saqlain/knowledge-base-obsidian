# Die Land Inside Circle Geometric Probability

## Statement

Suppose you drop a die at random on a rectangular region of 3 m × 2 m. What is the probability that it will land inside a circle of diameter 1 m situated inside the rectangle?

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 14.1, Question 20.

## Correct Answer

π/24 (area of circle = π/4 over area of rectangle = 6)
