# Probability of Not E Given 0.05

## Statement

If P(E) = 0.05, what is the probability of "not E"?

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 14.1, Question 5.

## Correct Answer

0.95

## Core Concept

[[Complementary Events]]

| label | weight | required | role |
