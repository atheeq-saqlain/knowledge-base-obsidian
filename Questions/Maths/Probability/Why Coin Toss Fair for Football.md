# Why Coin Toss Fair for Football

## Statement

Why is tossing a coin considered to be a fair way of deciding which team should get the ball at the beginning of a football game?

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 14.1, Question 3.

## Correct Answer

A fair coin has two equally likely outcomes (heads and tails), so each team has an equal chance of winning the toss.
