# Special Numbered Die Two Throws Probabilities

## Statement

A die is numbered in such a way that its faces show the numbers 1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 6. It is thrown two times and the total score in two throws is noted. Complete the score addition table, then find the probability that the total score is:

(i) even?

(ii) 6?

(iii) at least 6?

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 14.2 Optional, Question 2.

## Correct Answer

36 outcomes (6 × 6).
(i) 1/2
(ii) 4/36 = 1/9
(iii) 15/36 = 5/12
