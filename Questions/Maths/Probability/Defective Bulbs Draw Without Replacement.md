# Defective Bulbs Draw Without Replacement

## Statement

(i) A lot of 20 bulbs contains 4 defective ones. One bulb is drawn at random from the lot. What is the probability that this bulb is defective?

(ii) Suppose the bulb drawn in (i) is not defective and is not replaced. Now one bulb is drawn at random from the rest. What is the probability that this bulb is not defective?

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 14.1, Question 17.

## Correct Answer

(i) 1/5
(ii) 15/19
