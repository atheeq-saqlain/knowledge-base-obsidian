# Shyam Ekta Shop Visit Probabilities

## Statement

Two customers Shyam and Ekta are visiting a particular shop in the same week (Tuesday to Saturday). Each is equally likely to visit the shop on any day as on another day. What is the probability that both will visit the shop on:

(i) the same day?

(ii) consecutive days?

(iii) different days?

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 14.2 Optional, Question 1.

## Correct Answer

5 days; 25 equally likely outcomes.
(i) 1/5
(ii) 8/25
(iii) 4/5
