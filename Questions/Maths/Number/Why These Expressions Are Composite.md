# Why These Expressions Are Composite

## Statement

Explain why 7 × 11 × 13 + 13 and 7 × 6 × 5 × 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 + 5 are composite numbers.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 8.2, Question 6.

## Correct Answer

7 × 11 × 13 + 13 = 13(7 × 11 + 1) = 13 × 78, a product of two integers greater than 1.
7 × 6 × 5 × 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 + 5 = 5(7 × 6 × 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 + 1) = 5 × 1009, a product of two integers greater than 1.
