# HCF and LCM of Three Integers

## Statement

Find the LCM and HCF of the following integers by applying the prime factorisation method:
(i) 12, 15 and 21
(ii) 17, 23 and 29
(iii) 8, 9 and 25

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 8.2, Question 3.

## Correct Answer

(i) HCF = 3, LCM = 420
(ii) HCF = 1, LCM = 11339
(iii) HCF = 1, LCM = 1800
