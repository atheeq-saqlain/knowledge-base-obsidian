# Prime Factorisation of 140

## Statement

Express each number as a product of its prime factors:
(i) 140
(ii) 156
(iii) 3825
(iv) 5005
(v) 7429

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 8.2, Question 1.

## Correct Answer

(i) 140 = 2² × 5 × 7
(ii) 156 = 2² × 3 × 13
(iii) 3825 = 3² × 5² × 17
(iv) 5005 = 5 × 7 × 11 × 13
(v) 7429 = 17 × 19 × 23
