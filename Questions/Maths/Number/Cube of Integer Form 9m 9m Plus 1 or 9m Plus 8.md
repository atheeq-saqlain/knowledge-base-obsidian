# Cube of Integer Form 9m 9m Plus 1 or 9m Plus 8

## Statement

Use Euclid’s division lemma to show that the cube of any positive integer is of the form 9m, 9m + 1 or 9m + 8.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 8.1, Question 5.

## Correct Answer

Any positive integer a = 3q, 3q + 1 or 3q + 2.
- a = 3q ⇒ a³ = 27q³ = 9(3q³) = 9m
- a = 3q + 1 ⇒ a³ = 27q³ + 27q² + 9q + 1 = 9(…) + 1 = 9m + 1
- a = 3q + 2 ⇒ a³ = 27q³ + 54q² + 36q + 8 = 9(…) + 8 = 9m + 8
