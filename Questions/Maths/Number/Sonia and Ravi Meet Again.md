# Sonia and Ravi Meet Again

## Statement

Sonia takes 18 minutes to drive one round of a circular field, while Ravi takes 12 minutes for the same. They start at the same point and time, going in the same direction. After how many minutes will they meet again at the starting point?

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 8.2, Question 7.

## Correct Answer

36 minutes

LCM(18, 12) = 36.
