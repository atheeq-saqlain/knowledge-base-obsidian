# Circular Cover Equilateral Design

## Statement

In a circular table cover of radius 32 cm, a design is formed leaving an equilateral triangle ABC in the middle. Find the area of the design.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 5.3, Question 6.

## Correct Answer

Design = π×32² − area of equilateral triangle with circumradius 32 (side = 32√3). Standard textbook numerical value.
