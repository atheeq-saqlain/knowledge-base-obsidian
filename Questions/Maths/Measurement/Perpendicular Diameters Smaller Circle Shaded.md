# Perpendicular Diameters Smaller Circle Shaded

## Statement

AB and CD are two diameters of a circle (with centre O) perpendicular to each other, and OD is the diameter of the smaller circle. If OA = 7 cm, find the area of the shaded region.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 5.3, Question 9.

## Correct Answer

(3/4)π×7² + (1/2)π×(7/2)² = (343π)/8 cm² (matching the textbook shading).
