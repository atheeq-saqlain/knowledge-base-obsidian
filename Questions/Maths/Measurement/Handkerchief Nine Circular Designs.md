# Handkerchief Nine Circular Designs

## Statement

On a square handkerchief, nine circular designs each of radius 7 cm are made. Find the area of the remaining portion of the handkerchief.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 5.3, Question 11.

## Correct Answer

Handkerchief side 42 cm (3×14): 42² − 9π×7² = 1764 − 1386 = 378 cm².
