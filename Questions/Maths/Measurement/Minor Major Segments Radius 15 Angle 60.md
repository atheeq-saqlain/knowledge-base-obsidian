# Minor Major Segments Radius 15 Angle 60

## Statement

A chord of a circle of radius 15 cm subtends an angle of 60° at the centre. Find the areas of the corresponding minor and major segments of the circle (Use π = 3.14 and √3 = 1.73).

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 5.2, Question 6.

## Correct Answer

Minor ≈ 20.43 cm²; major ≈ 686.06 cm² (sector − equilateral triangle; circle − minor).
