# Square Semicircles Shaded Area

## Statement

Find the area of the shaded region if ABCD is a square of side 14 cm and APD and BPC are semicircles.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 5.3, Question 3.

## Correct Answer

Square − two semicircles = 14² − π×7² = 196 − 154 = 42 cm².
