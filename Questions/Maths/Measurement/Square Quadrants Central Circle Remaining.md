# Square Quadrants Central Circle Remaining

## Statement

From each corner of a square of side 4 cm, a quadrant of a circle of radius 1 cm is cut, and a central circle of diameter 2 cm is also cut. Find the area of the remaining portion of the square.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 5.3, Question 5.

## Correct Answer

4² − 4×(1/4)π(1)² − π(1)² = 16 − 2π cm².
