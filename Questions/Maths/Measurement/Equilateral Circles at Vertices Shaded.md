# Equilateral Circles at Vertices Shaded

## Statement

The area of an equilateral triangle ABC is 17320.5 cm². With each vertex of the triangle as centre, a circle is drawn with its radius equal to half the length of the side of the triangle. Find the area of the shaded region (Use π = 3.14 and √3 = 1.73205).

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 5.3, Question 10.

## Correct Answer

Find side from area; shaded = triangle − three sectors (or as in figure) using the given constants.
