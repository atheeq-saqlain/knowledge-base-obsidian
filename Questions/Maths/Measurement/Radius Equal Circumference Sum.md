# Radius Equal Circumference Sum

## Statement

The radii of two circles are 19 cm and 9 cm respectively. Find the radius of the circle which has circumference equal to the sum of the circumferences of the two circles. (Use π = 22/7 unless stated otherwise.)

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 5.1, Question 1.

## Correct Answer

28 cm

2πR = 2π(19) + 2π(9) ⇒ R = 28.
