# Racing Track Inner Perimeter Area

## Statement

A racing track has parallel straight segments connected by semicircular ends. The distance between the two inner parallel line segments is 60 m and they are each 106 m long. If the track is 10 m wide, find:

(i) the distance around the track along its inner edge
(ii) the total area of the track

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 5.3, Question 8.

## Correct Answer

(i) 2×106 + 2π×30 = 212 + 60π m
(ii) Outer − inner: 2×106×10 + π(40² − 30²) = 2120 + 700π m²
