# Quadrant OACB Shaded OD 2

## Statement

OACB is a quadrant of a circle with centre O and radius 3.5 cm. If OD = 2 cm, find the area of:

(i) quadrant OACB
(ii) the shaded region

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 5.3, Question 12.

## Correct Answer

(i) (1/4)π(3.5)² = 77/8 cm²
(ii) Quadrant − triangle ODC (as in the textbook figure).
