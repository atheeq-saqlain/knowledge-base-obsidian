# Silver Brooch Wire and Sectors

## Statement

A brooch is made with silver wire in the form of a circle with a diameter of 35 mm. The wire is also used to make 5 diameters which divide the circle into 10 equal sectors. Find:

(i) the total length of the silver wire required
(ii) the area of each sector of the brooch

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 5.2, Question 9.

## Correct Answer

(i) Circumference + 5 diameters = 35π + 5×35 = 110 + 175 = 285 mm (π = 22/7)
(ii) Each sector (36°) area = (36/360)π(17.5)² = 192.5 mm²
