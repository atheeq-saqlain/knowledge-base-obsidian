# Quadrant Semicircle on BC Shaded

## Statement

ABC is a quadrant of a circle of radius 14 cm and a semicircle is drawn with BC as its diameter. Find the area of the shaded region.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 5.3, Question 15.

## Correct Answer

Combine quadrant and semicircle (and triangle) as in the textbook shading; BC = 14√2.
