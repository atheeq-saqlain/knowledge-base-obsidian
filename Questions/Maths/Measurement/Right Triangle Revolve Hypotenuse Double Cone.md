# Right Triangle Revolve Hypotenuse Double Cone

## Statement

A right triangle, whose sides are 3 cm and 4 cm (other than hypotenuse), is made to revolve about its hypotenuse. Find the volume and surface area of the double cone so formed.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 15.5 (Optional), Question 2.
Unless stated otherwise, use π = 22/7.

## Correct Answer

Volume = 9.6π cm³; surface area = 16.8π cm²
