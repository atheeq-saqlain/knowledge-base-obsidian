# Shaded Concentric Sector Ring 40

## Statement

Find the area of the shaded region if the radii of two concentric circles with centre O are 7 cm and 14 cm respectively and ∠AOC = 40°.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 5.3, Question 2.

## Correct Answer

Ring sector: (40/360)π(14² − 7²) = 154/9 cm².
