# Square Four Corner Circles Shaded

## Statement

In a square ABCD of side 14 cm, four circles are drawn with centres A, B, C and D such that each circle touches externally two of the remaining three circles. Find the area of the shaded region.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 5.3, Question 7.

## Correct Answer

Square − four quarter-circles of r = 7: 196 − π×49 = 42 cm².
