# Minute Hand Area in 5 Minutes

## Statement

The length of the minute hand of a clock is 14 cm. Find the area swept by the minute hand in 5 minutes. (Use π = 22/7.)

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 5.2, Question 3.

## Correct Answer

154/3 cm²

In 5 minutes the hand turns (5/60)×360° = 30°. Area = (30/360)π(14)² = 154/3 cm².
