# Equilateral Arc Shaded Region

## Statement

Find the area of the shaded region where a circular arc of radius 6 cm has been drawn with vertex O of an equilateral triangle OAB of side 12 cm as its centre.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 5.3, Question 4.

## Correct Answer

Shaded = segment (or sector∓triangle) of 60° with r = 6 as in the textbook figure: typically (1/2)×6²(π/3 − √3/2) style.
