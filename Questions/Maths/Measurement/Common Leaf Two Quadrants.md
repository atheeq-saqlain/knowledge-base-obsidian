# Common Leaf Two Quadrants

## Statement

Calculate the area of the designed region common between two quadrants of circles of radius 8 cm each.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 5.3, Question 16.

## Correct Answer

Leaf = 2×(quarter circle − isosceles right triangle) = 2[(1/4)π×8² − (1/2)×8×8] = 32(π − 2) cm².
