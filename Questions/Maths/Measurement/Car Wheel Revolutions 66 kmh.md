# Car Wheel Revolutions 66 kmh

## Statement

The wheels of a car have a diameter of 80 cm each. How many complete revolutions does each wheel make in 10 minutes when the car is travelling at a speed of 66 km per hour?

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 5.1, Question 4.

## Correct Answer

Speed 66 km/h = 110000 cm/min; in 10 min distance = 1 100 000 cm. Circumference = 80π = 1760/7 cm (π = 22/7). Revolutions = 1 100 000 ÷ (1760/7) = 4375.
