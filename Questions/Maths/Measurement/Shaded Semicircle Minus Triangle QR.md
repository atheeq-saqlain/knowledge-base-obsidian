# Shaded Semicircle Minus Triangle QR

## Statement

Find the area of the shaded region in the given figure: circle with centre O; PQ = 24 cm, PR = 7 cm; QR is the diameter (∠QPR = 90°).

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 5.3, Question 1.

## Correct Answer

Diameter QR = 25 cm (3-4-5 scaled). Shaded = semicircle on QR minus ΔPQR: (1/2)π(12.5)² − (1/2)×7×24.
