# Concentric Arcs Shaded 30 Degrees

## Statement

AB and CD are respectively arcs of two concentric circles of radii 21 cm and 7 cm with centre O. If ∠AOB = 30°, find the area of the shaded region.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 5.3, Question 14.

## Correct Answer

(30/360)π(21² − 7²) = 154/3 cm².
