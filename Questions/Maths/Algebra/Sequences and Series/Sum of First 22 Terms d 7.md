# Sum of First 22 Terms d 7

## Statement

Find the sum of first 22 terms of an AP in which d = 7 and the 22nd term is 149.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 1.3, Question 7.

## Correct Answer

1661

a₂₂ = 149 ⇒ a + 21×7 = 149 ⇒ a = 2.
S₂₂ = (22/2)[4 + 21×7] = 11×151 = 1661.
