# Sum of First 15 Multiples of 8

## Statement

Find the sum of the first 15 multiples of 8.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 1.3, Question 13.

## Correct Answer

960

The AP is 8, 16, …, 120. S₁₅ = (15/2)(8 + 120) = 960.
