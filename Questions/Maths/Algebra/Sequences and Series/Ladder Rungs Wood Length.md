# Ladder Rungs Wood Length

## Statement

A ladder has rungs 25 cm apart (Fig. 1.7). The rungs decrease uniformly in length from 45 cm at the bottom to 25 cm at the top. If the top and the bottom rungs are 2½ m apart, what is the length of the wood required for the rungs?

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 1.4 (optional), Question 3.

## Correct Answer

385 cm

Distance between top and bottom rungs = 250 cm, spacing 25 cm, so number of rungs = 250/25 + 1 = 11.
Lengths form an AP with a = 45, l = 25, n = 11.
S = (11/2)(45 + 25) = 385 cm.
