# AP with Third Term 16

## Statement

Determine the AP whose third term is 16 and the 7th term exceeds the 5th term by 12.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 1.2, Question 16.

## Correct Answer

4, 10, 16, 22, …

a₃ = 16 ⇒ a + 2d = 16.
a₇ − a₅ = 12 ⇒ 2d = 12 ⇒ d = 6, then a = 4.
