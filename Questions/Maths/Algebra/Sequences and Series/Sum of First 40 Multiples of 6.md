# Sum of First 40 Multiples of 6

## Statement

Find the sum of the first 40 positive integers divisible by 6.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 1.3, Question 12.

## Correct Answer

4920

The AP is 6, 12, …, 240. S₄₀ = (40/2)(6 + 240) = 20×246 = 4920.
