# 11th Term of AP -3 Half 2

## Statement

Choose the correct option and justify:

The 11th term of the AP: −3, −1/2, 2, … is

(A) 28  (B) 22  (C) −38  (D) −48½

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 1.2, Question 2(ii).

## Correct Answer

(B) 22

Here a = −3, d = −1/2 − (−3) = 5/2, n = 11.
a₁₁ = −3 + 10 × (5/2) = −3 + 25 = 22.
