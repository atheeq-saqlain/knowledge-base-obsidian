# Subba Rao Salary Reach 7000

## Statement

Subba Rao started work in 1995 at an annual salary of ₹5000 and received an increment of ₹200 each year. In which year did his income reach ₹7000?

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 1.2, Question 19.

## Correct Answer

In the 11th year (year 2005).

Salaries form AP with a = 5000, d = 200, aₙ = 7000.
7000 = 5000 + (n − 1)×200 ⇒ n − 1 = 10 ⇒ n = 11.
Starting in 1995, the 11th year is 2005.
