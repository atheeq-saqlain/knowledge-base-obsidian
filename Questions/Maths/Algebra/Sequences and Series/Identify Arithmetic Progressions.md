# Identify Arithmetic Progressions

## Statement

In which of the following situations does the list of numbers involved make an arithmetic progression, and why?

(i) The taxi fare after each km when the fare is ₹15 for the first km and ₹8 for each additional km.

(ii) The amount of air present in a cylinder when a vacuum pump removes 1/4 of the air remaining in the cylinder at a time.

(iii) The cost of digging a well after every metre of digging, when it costs ₹150 for the first metre and rises by ₹50 for each subsequent metre.

(iv) The amount of money in the account every year, when ₹10000 is deposited at compound interest at 8% per annum.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 1.1, Question 1.

## Correct Answer

(i) Yes. Fares 15, 23, 31, … form an AP (common difference 8).

(ii) No. Remaining volumes are V, (3/4)V, (3/4)²V, … (geometric, not arithmetic).

(iii) Yes. Costs 150, 200, 250, … form an AP (common difference 50).

(iv) No. Amounts grow by a constant ratio (compound interest), not a constant difference.
