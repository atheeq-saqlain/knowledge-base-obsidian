# 17th Term Exceeds 10th by 7

## Statement

The 17th term of an AP exceeds its 10th term by 7. Find the common difference.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 1.2, Question 10.

## Correct Answer

d = 1

a₁₇ − a₁₀ = 7 ⇒ [a + 16d] − [a + 9d] = 7 ⇒ 7d = 7 ⇒ d = 1.
