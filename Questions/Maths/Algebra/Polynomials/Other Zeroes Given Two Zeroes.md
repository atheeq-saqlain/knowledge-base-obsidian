# Other Zeroes Given Two Zeroes

## Statement

Obtain all other zeroes of 3x⁴ + 6x³ − 2x² − 10x − 5, if two of its zeroes are √(5/3) and −√(5/3).

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 9.3, Question 3.

## Correct Answer

−1 and −1 (a repeated zero)

Factor from the given zeroes: 3x² − 5. Division gives quotient x² + 2x + 1 = (x + 1)².
