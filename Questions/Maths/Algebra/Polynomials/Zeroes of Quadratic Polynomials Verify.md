# Zeroes of Quadratic Polynomials Verify

## Statement

Find the zeroes of the following quadratic polynomials and verify the relationship between the zeroes and the coefficients:

(i) x² − 2x − 8
(ii) 4s² − 4s + 1
(iii) 6x² − 3 − 7x
(iv) 4u² + 8u
(v) t² − 15
(vi) 3x² − x − 4

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 9.2, Question 1.

## Correct Answer

(i) 4, −2; sum 2 = −b/a, product −8 = c/a
(ii) 1/2, 1/2; sum 1 = −b/a, product 1/4 = c/a
(iii) −1/3, 3/2; sum 7/6 = −b/a, product −1/2 = c/a
(iv) 0, −2; sum −2 = −b/a, product 0 = c/a
(v) √15, −√15; sum 0 = −b/a, product −15 = c/a
(vi) 4/3, −1; sum 1/3 = −b/a, product −4/3 = c/a
