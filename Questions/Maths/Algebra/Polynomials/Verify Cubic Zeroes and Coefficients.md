# Verify Cubic Zeroes and Coefficients

## Statement

Verify that the numbers given alongside each of the cubic polynomials below are their zeroes. Also verify the relationship between the zeroes and the coefficients:

(i) 2x³ + x² − 5x + 2; 1/2, 1, −2
(ii) x³ − 4x² + 5x − 2; 2, 1, 1

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 9.4 (Optional), Question 1.

## Correct Answer

(i) Each value gives p = 0. Sum = −1/2 = −b/a; sum of products two at a time = −5/2 = c/a; product = −1 = −d/a.
(ii) Each value gives p = 0. Sum = 4 = −b/a; sum of products two at a time = 5 = c/a; product = 2 = −d/a.
