# Zeroes in AP Find a and b

## Statement

If the zeroes of the polynomial x³ − 3x² + x + 1 are a − b, a and a + b, find a and b.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 9.4 (Optional), Question 3.

## Correct Answer

a = 1, b = ±√2

Sum of zeroes: 3a = 3 ⇒ a = 1. Sum of products two at a time: 3a² − b² = 1 ⇒ b² = 2.
