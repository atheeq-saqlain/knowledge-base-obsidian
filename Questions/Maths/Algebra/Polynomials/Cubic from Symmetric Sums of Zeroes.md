# Cubic from Symmetric Sums of Zeroes

## Statement

Find a cubic polynomial with the sum, sum of the product of its zeroes taken two at a time, and the product of its zeroes as 2, −7 and −14 respectively.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 9.4 (Optional), Question 2.

## Correct Answer

x³ − 2x² − 7x + 14 (or any non-zero constant multiple)
