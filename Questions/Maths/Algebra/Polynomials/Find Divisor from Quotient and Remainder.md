# Find Divisor from Quotient and Remainder

## Statement

On dividing x³ − 3x² + x + 2 by a polynomial g(x), the quotient and remainder were x − 2 and −2x + 4 respectively. Find g(x).

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 9.3, Question 4.

## Correct Answer

g(x) = x² − x + 1

From p = g·q + r: g = (p − r)/q = (x³ − 3x² + 3x − 2)/(x − 2) = x² − x + 1.
