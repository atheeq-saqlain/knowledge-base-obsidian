# Quadratic from Sum and Product of Zeroes

## Statement

Find a quadratic polynomial each with the given numbers as the sum and product of its zeroes respectively:

(i) 1/4, −1
(ii) √2, 1/3
(iii) 0, √5
(iv) 1, 1
(v) −1/4, 1/4
(vi) 4, 1

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 9.2, Question 2.

## Correct Answer

Any non-zero constant multiple is acceptable; one choice each:

(i) x² − (1/4)x − 1, or 4x² − x − 4
(ii) x² − √2 x + 1/3, or 3x² − 3√2 x + 1
(iii) x² + √5
(iv) x² − x + 1
(v) x² + (1/4)x + 1/4, or 4x² + x + 1
(vi) x² − 4x + 1
