# Express and Passenger Train Speeds

## Statement

An express train takes 1 hour less than a passenger train to travel 132 km between Mysore and Bangalore. If the average speed of the express train is 11 km/h more than that of the passenger train, find the average speeds of the two trains.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 10.3, Question 10.

## Correct Answer

Passenger train 33 km/h; express train 44 km/h
