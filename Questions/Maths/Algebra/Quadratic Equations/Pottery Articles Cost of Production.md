# Pottery Articles Cost of Production

## Statement

A cottage industry produces a certain number of pottery articles in a day. On a particular day the cost of production of each article was ₹3 more than twice the number of articles produced. If the total cost of production was ₹90, find the number of articles produced and the cost of each article.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 10.2, Question 6.

## Correct Answer

6 articles at ₹15 each
