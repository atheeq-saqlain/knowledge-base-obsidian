# Two Friends Ages Product 48

## Statement

Is the following situation possible? If so, determine their present ages.

The sum of the ages of two friends is 20 years. Four years ago, the product of their ages in years was 48.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 10.4, Question 4.

## Correct Answer

Not possible — the resulting quadratic has D = −48 < 0.
