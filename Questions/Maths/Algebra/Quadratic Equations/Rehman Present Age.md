# Rehman Present Age

## Statement

The sum of the reciprocals of Rehman’s ages (in years) 3 years ago and 5 years from now is 1/3. Find his present age.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 10.3, Question 4.

## Correct Answer

7 years

[[Quadratic Formula]]
