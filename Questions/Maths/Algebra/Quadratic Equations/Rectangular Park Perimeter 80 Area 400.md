# Rectangular Park Perimeter 80 Area 400

## Statement

Is it possible to design a rectangular park of perimeter 80 m and area 400 m²? If so, find its length and breadth.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 10.4, Question 5.

## Correct Answer

Yes. Length = breadth = 20 m (a square; D = 0 for t² − 40t + 400 = 0).
