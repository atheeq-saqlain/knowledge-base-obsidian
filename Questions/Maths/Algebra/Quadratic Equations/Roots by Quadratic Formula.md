# Roots by Quadratic Formula

## Statement

Find the roots of the quadratic equations given in Exercise 10.3 Question 1 by applying the quadratic formula.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 10.3, Question 2.

## Correct Answer

(i) 3, 1/2
(ii) (−1 ± √33)/4
(iii) −√3/2, −√3/2
(iv) No real roots (D < 0)
