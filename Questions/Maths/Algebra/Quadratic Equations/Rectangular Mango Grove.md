# Rectangular Mango Grove

## Statement

Is it possible to design a rectangular mango grove whose length is twice its breadth, and whose area is 800 m²? If so, find its length and breadth.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 10.4, Question 3.

## Correct Answer

Yes. Breadth 20 m, length 40 m (D > 0 for 2x² − 800 = 0).
