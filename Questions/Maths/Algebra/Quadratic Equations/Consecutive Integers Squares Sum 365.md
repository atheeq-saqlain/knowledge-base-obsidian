# Consecutive Integers Squares Sum 365

## Statement

Find two consecutive positive integers, the sum of whose squares is 365.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 10.2, Question 4.

## Correct Answer

13 and 14
