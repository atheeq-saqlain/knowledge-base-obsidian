# Ritu Rowing Downstream Upstream

## Statement

Ritu can row downstream 20 km in 2 hours, and upstream 4 km in 2 hours. Find her speed of rowing in still water and the speed of the current.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 3.6, Question 2(i).

## Correct Answer

Still water 6 km/h; current 4 km/h.
