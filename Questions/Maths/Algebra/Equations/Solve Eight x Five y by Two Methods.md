# Solve Eight x Five y by Two Methods

## Statement

Solve the following pair of linear equations by the substitution and cross-multiplication methods:

8x + 5y = 9 and 3x + 2y = 4

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 3.5, Question 3.

## Correct Answer

x = −2, y = 5
