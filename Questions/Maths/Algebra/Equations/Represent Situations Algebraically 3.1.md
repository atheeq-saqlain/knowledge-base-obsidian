# Represent Situations Algebraically 3.1

## Statement

Represent each situation algebraically (and geometrically / graphically as asked in the textbook):

1. Seven years ago, Aftab was seven times as old as his daughter. Three years from now, he will be three times as old as she will be.

2. A coach buys 3 bats and 6 balls for ₹3900. Later she buys another bat and 3 more balls of the same kind for ₹1300.

3. The cost of 2 kg of apples and 1 kg of grapes was ₹160. After a month, the cost of 4 kg of apples and 2 kg of grapes is ₹300.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 3.1.

## Correct Answer

1. Let present ages be s (Aftab) and t (daughter): s − 7 = 7(t − 7) and s + 3 = 3(t + 3), i.e. s − 7t + 42 = 0 and s − 3t − 6 = 0. Graphically the lines intersect at (42, 12).

2. Let bat cost x and ball cost y: 3x + 6y = 3900 and x + 3y = 1300.

3. Let apple cost per kg be x and grapes per kg be y: 2x + y = 160 and 4x + 2y = 300. Here a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂, so the lines are parallel (inconsistent).
