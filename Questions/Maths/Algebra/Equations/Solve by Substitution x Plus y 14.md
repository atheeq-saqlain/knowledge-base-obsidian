# Solve by Substitution x Plus y 14

## Statement

Solve the following pair of linear equations by the substitution method:

x + y = 14  
x − y = 4

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 3.3, Question 1(i).

## Correct Answer

x = 9, y = 5

From the second equation, x = 4 + y. Substitute: (4 + y) + y = 14 ⇒ 2y = 10 ⇒ y = 5, x = 9.
