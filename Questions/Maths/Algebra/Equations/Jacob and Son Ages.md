# Jacob and Son Ages

## Statement

Five years hence, the age of Jacob will be three times that of his son. Five years ago, Jacob’s age was seven times that of his son. What are their present ages?

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 3.3, Question 3(vi).

## Correct Answer

Jacob 40 years, son 10 years.

x − 3y − 10 = 0 and x − 7y + 30 = 0 (present ages x, y).
