# Supplementary Angles Differ by 18

## Statement

The larger of two supplementary angles exceeds the smaller by 18°. Find them.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 3.3, Question 3(ii).

## Correct Answer

99° and 81°.

x + y = 180, x − y = 18 ⇒ x = 99, y = 81.
