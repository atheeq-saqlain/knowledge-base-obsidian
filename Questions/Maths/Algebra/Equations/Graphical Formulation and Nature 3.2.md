# Graphical Formulation and Nature 3.2

## Statement

1. Formulate and solve graphically:
   (i) 10 Class X students took part in a quiz; girls are 4 more than boys. Find the numbers of boys and girls.
   (ii) 5 pencils and 7 pens cost ₹50; 7 pencils and 5 pens cost ₹46. Find the cost of one pencil and one pen.

2–3. (See [[Intersecting Parallel or Coincident Lines]] and [[Consistent or Inconsistent Pairs]].)

4. Solve graphically if consistent:
   (i) x + y = 5; 2x + 2y = 10
   (ii) x − y = 8; 3x − 3y = 16
   (iii) 2x + y − 6 = 0; 4x − 2y − 4 = 0
   (iv) 2x − 2y − 2 = 0; 4x − 4y − 5 = 0

5. (See [[Rectangular Garden Dimensions]].)

6. Given 2x + 3y − 8 = 0, write another equation so the pair represents (i) intersecting (ii) parallel (iii) coincident lines.

7. Draw x − y + 1 = 0 and 3x + 2y − 12 = 0. Find the vertices of the triangle formed with the x-axis and shade the region.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 3.2.

## Correct Answer

1. (i) x + y = 10, y = x + 4 ⇒ boys 3, girls 7. (ii) 5x + 7y = 50, 7x + 5y = 46 ⇒ pencil ₹3, pen ₹5.

4. (i) Coincident — infinitely many solutions. (ii) Parallel — inconsistent. (iii) Unique solution x = 2, y = 2. (iv) Parallel — inconsistent.

6. Examples: (i) x + y − 1 = 0 (a₁/a₂ ≠ b₁/b₂); (ii) 4x + 6y − 9 = 0 (a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂); (iii) 4x + 6y − 16 = 0 (all three ratios equal).

7. Lines meet x-axis at (−1, 0) and (4, 0); they intersect at (2, 3). Triangle vertices (−1, 0), (4, 0), (2, 3).
