# Area of Sector Radius 6 Angle 60

## Statement

Find the area of a sector of a circle with radius 6 cm if angle of the sector is 60°. (Use π = 22/7.)

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 5.2, Question 1.

## Correct Answer

132/7 cm²

Area = (60/360)π(6)² = (1/6)×(22/7)×36 = 132/7 cm².
