# 30th Term of AP 10 7 4

## Statement

Choose the correct option and justify:

The 30th term of the AP: 10, 7, 4, … is

(A) 97  (B) 77  (C) −77  (D) −87

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 1.2, Question 2(i).

## Correct Answer

(C) −77

Here a = 10, d = −3, n = 30.
a₃₀ = 10 + (30 − 1)(−3) = 10 − 87 = −77.
