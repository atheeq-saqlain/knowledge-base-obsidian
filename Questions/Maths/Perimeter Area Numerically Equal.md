# Perimeter Area Numerically Equal

## Statement

If the perimeter and the area of a circle are numerically equal, then the radius of the circle is

(A) 2 units (B) π units (C) 4 units (D) 7 units

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 5.1, Question 5.

## Correct Answer

(A) 2 units

2πr = πr² ⇒ r = 2 (r ≠ 0).
