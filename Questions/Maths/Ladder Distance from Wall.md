# Ladder Distance from Wall

## Statement

A ladder 10 m long reaches a window 8 m above the ground. Find the distance of the foot of the ladder from the base of the wall.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 2.5, Question 9.

## Correct Answer

6 m

By Pythagoras Theorem: distance² + 8² = 10² ⇒ distance² = 100 − 64 = 36 ⇒ distance = 6 m.
