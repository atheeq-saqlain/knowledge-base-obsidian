# Midpoints of Rectangle Form Rhombus

## Statement

Rectangle A(−1, −1), B(−1, 4), C(5, 4), D(5, −1). Mid-points P, Q, R, S of AB, BC, CD, DA. Is PQRS a square, rectangle, or rhombus?

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 7.4, Question 8 (Optional).

## Correct Answer

All sides equal, diagonals unequal ⇒ rhombus (not a square).
