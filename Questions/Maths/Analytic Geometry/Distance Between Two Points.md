# Distance Between Two Points

## Statement

Find the distance between the following pairs of points:
(i) (2, 3) and (4, 1);
(ii) (−5, 7) and (−1, 3);
(iii) (a, b) and (−a, −b).

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 7.1, Question 1.

## Correct Answer

(i) 2√2.
(ii) 4√2.
(iii) 2√(a² + b²).
