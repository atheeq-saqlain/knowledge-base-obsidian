# Medians Intersect at Centroid

## Statement

Vertices A(4, 2), B(6, 5), C(1, 4).
(i) Median from A meets BC at D; find D.
(ii) Find P on AD with AP : PD = 2 : 1.
(iii) Find Q on median BE and R on CF with BQ : QE = CR : RF = 2 : 1.
(iv) What do you observe?

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 7.4, Question 7 (Optional).

## Correct Answer

D(7/2, 9/2); P = Q = R = (11/3, 11/3) (the centroid).
