# Area of Triangle from Vertices

## Statement

Find the area of the triangle whose vertices are (2, 3), (−1, 0) and (2, −4).

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 7.3, Question 1(i).

## Correct Answer

21/2 sq units

(1/2)|2(0+4) + (−1)(−4−3) + 2(3−0)| = (1/2)|8 + 7 + 6| = 21/2.
