# Area of Triangle from Vertices

## Statement

Find the area of the triangle whose vertices are:
(i) (2, 3), (−1, 0) and (2, −4);
(ii) (−5, −1), (3, −5) and (5, 2).

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 7.3, Question 1.

## Correct Answer

(i) 21/2 sq units.
(ii) 32 sq units.
