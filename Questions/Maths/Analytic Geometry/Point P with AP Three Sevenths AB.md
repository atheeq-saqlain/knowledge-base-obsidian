# Point P with AP Three Sevenths AB

## Statement

If A(−2, −2) and B(2, −4), find P on AB such that AP = (3/7) AB.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 7.2, Question 8.

## Correct Answer

P divides AB in 3 : 4; P(−2/7, −20/7).
