# Midpoint Triangle Area Ratio

## Statement

Find the area of the triangle formed by joining the mid-points of the sides of the triangle with vertices (0, −1), (2, 1) and (0, 3). Find the ratio of this area to the area of the given triangle.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 7.3, Question 3.

## Correct Answer

Mid-point triangle area = 1; original area = 4; ratio 1 : 4.
