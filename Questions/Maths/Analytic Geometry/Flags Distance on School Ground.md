# Flags Distance on School Ground

## Statement

On a rectangular ground with chalk lines 1 m apart, take A as origin with AB along the x-axis and AD along the y-axis (AD = 100 m). Niharika posts a green flag at 1/4 of AD on the 2nd line; Preet posts a red flag at 1/5 of AD on the 8th line. Find the distance between the flags and the position for a blue flag halfway between them.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 7.2, Question 3.

## Correct Answer

Green (2, 25), red (8, 20); distance √61 m; blue midpoint (5, 22.5).
