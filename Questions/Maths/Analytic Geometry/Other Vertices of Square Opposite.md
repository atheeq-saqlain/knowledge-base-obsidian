# Other Vertices of Square Opposite

## Statement

The opposite vertices of a square are (−1, 2) and (3, 2). Find the other two vertices.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 7.4, Question 4 (Optional).

## Correct Answer

(1, 4) and (1, 0).
