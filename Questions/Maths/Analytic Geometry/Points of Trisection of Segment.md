# Points of Trisection of Segment

## Statement

Find the coordinates of the points of trisection of the segment joining (4, −1) and (−2, −3).

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 7.2, Question 2.

## Correct Answer

(2, −5/3) and (0, −7/3).
