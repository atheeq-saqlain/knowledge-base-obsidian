# Isosceles Triangle from Three Points

## Statement

Check whether (5, −2), (6, 4) and (7, −2) are the vertices of an isosceles triangle.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 7.1, Question 4.

## Correct Answer

Yes; AB = BC = √37 (base AC = 2).
