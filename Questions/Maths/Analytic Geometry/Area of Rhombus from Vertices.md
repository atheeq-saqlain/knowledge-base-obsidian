# Area of Rhombus from Vertices

## Statement

Find the area of the rhombus with vertices (3, 0), (4, 5), (−1, 4) and (−2, −1) taken in order.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 7.2, Question 10.

## Correct Answer

(1/2) × d₁ × d₂ = (1/2) × 4√2 × 6√2 = 24 sq units.
