# Triangular Lawn Coordinates and Areas

## Statement

In the textbook rectangular plot with a triangular lawn PQR: taking A as origin, then taking C as origin, find the coordinates of P, Q, R in each case, compute both areas, and state what you observe.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part I, Exercise 7.4, Question 5 (Optional).

## Correct Answer

Areas from both choices of origin are equal (area is translation-invariant); read P, Q, R from the textbook figure for each origin.
