# Balloon Distance Travelled

## Statement

A 1.2 m tall girl spots a balloon moving with the wind in a horizontal line at a height of 88.2 m from the ground. The angle of elevation of the balloon from her eyes is 60°; after some time it reduces to 30°. Find the distance travelled by the balloon during the interval.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 12.1, Question 14.

## Correct Answer

58√3 m
