# All Ratios from Sec 13 over 12

## Statement

Given sec θ = 13/12, calculate all other trigonometric ratios of θ.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 11.1, Question 5.

## Correct Answer

sin θ = 5/13, cos θ = 12/13, tan θ = 5/12, csc θ = 13/5, cot θ = 12/5
