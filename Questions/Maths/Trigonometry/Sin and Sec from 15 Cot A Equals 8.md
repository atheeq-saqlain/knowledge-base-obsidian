# Sin and Sec from 15 Cot A Equals 8

## Statement

Given 15 cot A = 8, find sin A and sec A.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 11.1, Question 4.

## Correct Answer

sin A = 15/17, sec A = 17/8
