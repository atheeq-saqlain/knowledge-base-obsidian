# Prove Acute Cos A Equals Cos B Implies A Equals B

## Statement

If ∠A and ∠B are acute angles such that cos A = cos B, then prove that ∠A = ∠B.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 11.1, Question 6.

## Correct Answer

In right triangles with equal cosines, adjacent/hypotenuse ratios match; constructing equal adjacent and hypotenuse sides yields congruent triangles (or use that cosine is one-to-one and strictly decreasing on (0°, 90°)). Hence A = B.
