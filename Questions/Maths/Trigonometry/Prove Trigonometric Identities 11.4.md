# Prove Trigonometric Identities 11.4

## Statement

Prove the following identities (angles acute and expressions defined):

(i) (csc θ − cot θ)² = (1 − cos θ)/(1 + cos θ)
(ii) cos A/(1 + sin A) + (1 + sin A)/cos A = 2 sec A
(iii) tan θ/(1 − cot θ) + cot θ/(1 − tan θ) = 1 + sec θ csc θ
(iv) (1 + sec A)/sec A = sin² A/(1 − cos A)
(v) (cos A − sin A + 1)/(cos A + sin A − 1) = csc A + cot A, using csc² A = 1 + cot² A
(vi) √[(1 + sin A)/(1 − sin A)] = sec A + tan A
(vii) (sin θ − 2 sin³ θ)/(2 cos³ θ − cos θ) = tan θ
(viii) (sin A + csc A)² + (cos A + sec A)² = 7 + tan² A + cot² A
(ix) (csc A − sin A)(sec A − cos A) = 1/(tan A + cot A)
(x) [(1 + tan² A)/(1 + cot² A)] = [(1 − tan A)/(1 − cot A)]² = tan² A

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 11.4, Question 5.

## Correct Answer

Each identity holds. Typical approach: express in sin and cos (or divide numerator/denominator by a suitable factor) and apply sin² + cos² = 1, 1 + tan² = sec², 1 + cot² = csc².
