# TV Tower on Canal Bank

## Statement

A TV tower stands vertically on a bank of a canal. From a point on the other bank directly opposite the tower, the angle of elevation of the top is 60°. From another point 20 m away from this point on the line joining this point to the foot of the tower, the angle of elevation is 30°. Find the height of the tower and the width of the canal.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 12.1, Question 11.

## Correct Answer

Height 10√3 m; width of canal 10 m
