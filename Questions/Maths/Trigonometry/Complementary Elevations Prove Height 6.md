# Complementary Elevations Prove Height 6

## Statement

The angles of elevation of the top of a tower from two points at distances 4 m and 9 m from the base of the tower and in the same straight line with it are complementary. Prove that the height of the tower is 6 m.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 12.1, Question 16.

## Correct Answer

Let height = h and the angles be θ and 90° − θ. Then tan θ = h/4 and tan(90° − θ) = cot θ = h/9. So (h/4)(h/9) = tan θ · cot θ = 1 ⇒ h² = 36 ⇒ h = 6 m (height positive).
