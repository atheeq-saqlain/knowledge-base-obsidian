# Cos and Tan from Sin 3 over 4

## Statement

If sin A = 3/4, calculate cos A and tan A.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 11.1, Question 3.

## Correct Answer

cos A = √7/4, tan A = 3/√7 (acute A)
