# Two Equal Poles Across a Road

## Statement

Two poles of equal heights stand opposite each other on either side of an 80 m wide road. From a point between them on the road, the angles of elevation of the tops of the poles are 60° and 30° respectively. Find the height of the poles and the distances of the point from the poles.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 12.1, Question 10.

## Correct Answer

Height 20√3 m; distances 20 m and 60 m from the point
