# Car Approaching Tower Time

## Statement

A straight highway leads to the foot of a tower. A man at the top observes a car at an angle of depression of 30°. Six seconds later the angle of depression is 60°. Find the time taken by the car to reach the foot of the tower from this latter point.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 12.1, Question 15.

## Correct Answer

3 seconds

[[Angle of Depression]]
