# Express Sin 67 Plus Cos 75 Below 45

## Statement

Express sin 67° + cos 75° in terms of trigonometric ratios of angles between 0° and 45°.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 11.3, Question 7.

## Correct Answer

cos 23° + sin 15°
