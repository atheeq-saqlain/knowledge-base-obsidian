# Ratios in Triangle AB 24 BC 7

## Statement

In ΔABC, right-angled at B, AB = 24 cm and BC = 7 cm. Determine:
(i) sin A, cos A
(ii) sin C, cos C

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 11.1, Question 1.

## Correct Answer

AC = 25 cm.
(i) sin A = 7/25, cos A = 24/25
(ii) sin C = 24/25, cos C = 7/25
