# Sin Cos Tan P in Triangle PR Plus QR 25

## Statement

In ΔPQR, right-angled at Q, PR + QR = 25 cm and PQ = 5 cm. Determine sin P, cos P and tan P.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 11.1, Question 10.

## Correct Answer

sin P = 12/13, cos P = 5/13, tan P = 12/5

(QR = 12 cm, PR = 13 cm.)
