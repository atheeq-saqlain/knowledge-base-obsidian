# Two Slides Lengths in a Park

## Statement

A contractor plans two slides in a park: one of height 1.5 m inclined at 30° to the ground (for children below 5), and a steep slide of height 3 m inclined at 60° (for elder children). Find the length of the slide in each case.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 12.1, Question 3.

## Correct Answer

3 m and 2√3 m

## Core Concept

[[Angle of Elevation]]
