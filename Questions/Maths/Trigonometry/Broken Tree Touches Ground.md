# Broken Tree Touches Ground

## Statement

A tree breaks due to storm and the broken part bends so that the top of the tree touches the ground making an angle of 30° with it. The distance between the foot of the tree and the point where the top touches the ground is 8 m. Find the height of the tree.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 12.1, Question 2.

## Correct Answer

8√3 m

(Broken part = 16 m; remaining stump = 8/√3 m; total height = 8√3 m.)
