# Express Sin Sec Tan in Terms of Cot

## Statement

Express the trigonometric ratios sin A, sec A and tan A in terms of cot A.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 11.4, Question 1.

## Correct Answer

sin A = 1 / √(1 + cot² A)
sec A = √(1 + cot² A) / cot A
tan A = 1 / cot A
(taking positive roots for acute A)
