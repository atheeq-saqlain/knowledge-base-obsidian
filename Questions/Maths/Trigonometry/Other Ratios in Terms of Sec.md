# Other Ratios in Terms of Sec

## Statement

Write all the other trigonometric ratios of ∠A in terms of sec A.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 11.4, Question 2.

## Correct Answer

cos A = 1/sec A
sin A = √(sec² A − 1) / sec A
tan A = √(sec² A − 1)
cot A = 1 / √(sec² A − 1)
csc A = sec A / √(sec² A − 1)
(positive roots for acute A)
