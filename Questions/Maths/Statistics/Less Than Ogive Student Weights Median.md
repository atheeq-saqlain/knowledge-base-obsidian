# Less Than Ogive Student Weights Median

## Statement

Plot a less-than ogive for the weights of 35 students given as less than 38, 40, 42, 44, 46, 48, 50, 52 kg with cumulative frequencies 0, 3, 5, 9, 14, 28, 32, 35. Locate the median from the graph and verify it mathematically.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 13.4, Question 2.

## Correct Answer

Median = 46.5 kg (from the formula in class 46–48; the graph should give the same abscissa at cf = 17.5).
