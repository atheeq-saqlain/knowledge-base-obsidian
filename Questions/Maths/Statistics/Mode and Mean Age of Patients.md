# Mode and Mean Age of Patients

## Statement

Find the mode and mean age of patients admitted during a year: ages 5–15, 15–25, 25–35, 35–45, 45–55, 55–65 with frequencies 6, 11, 21, 23, 14, 5.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 13.2, Question 1.

## Correct Answer

Mode ≈ 36.8 years; mean ≈ 35.37 years
