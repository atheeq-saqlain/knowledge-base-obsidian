# Median Weight of Students

## Statement

Find the median weight of 30 students: weights 40–45, 45–50, 50–55, 55–60, 60–65, 65–70, 70–75 kg with frequencies 2, 3, 8, 6, 6, 3, 2.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 13.3, Question 7.

## Correct Answer

≈ 56.67 kg
