# Mean Days Student Absent

## Statement

A class teacher has the absentee record of 40 students for a full term: number of days 0–6, 6–10, 10–14, 14–20, 20–28, 28–38, 38–40 with frequencies 11, 10, 7, 4, 4, 3, 1. Find the mean number of days a student was absent.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 13.1, Question 8.

## Correct Answer

12.48 days (approx.)
