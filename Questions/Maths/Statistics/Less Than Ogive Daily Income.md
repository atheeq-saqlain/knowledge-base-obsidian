# Less Than Ogive Daily Income

## Statement

Convert the daily income distribution of 50 factory workers into a less-than cumulative frequency distribution and draw its ogive: income (₹) 100–120, 120–140, 140–160, 160–180, 180–200 with frequencies 12, 14, 8, 6, 10.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 13.4, Question 1.

## Correct Answer

Less-than cf: <120: 12; <140: 26; <160: 34; <180: 40; <200: 50. Plot upper limits against these cf values to obtain the less-than ogive.
