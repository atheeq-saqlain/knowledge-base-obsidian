# Modal Monthly Expenditure Families

## Statement

Find the modal monthly expenditure of 200 families: expenditure (₹) 1000–1500, 1500–2000, 2000–2500, 2500–3000, 3000–3500, 3500–4000, 4000–4500, 4500–5000 with frequencies 24, 40, 33, 28, 30, 22, 16, 7.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 13.2, Question 3.

## Correct Answer

≈ ₹1847.83
