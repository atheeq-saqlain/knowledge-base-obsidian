# Mean SO2 Concentration

## Statement

Calculate the mean concentration of SO₂ (in ppm) across 30 localities for classes 0.00–0.04, 0.04–0.08, 0.08–0.12, 0.12–0.16, 0.16–0.20, 0.20–0.24 with frequencies 4, 9, 9, 2, 4, 2.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 13.1, Question 7.

## Correct Answer

0.099 ppm (approx.)
