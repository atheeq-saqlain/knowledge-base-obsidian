# Median Lifetime Neon Lamps

## Statement

Find the median lifetime of 400 neon lamps: lifetimes 1500–2000, 2000–2500, 2500–3000, 3000–3500, 3500–4000, 4000–4500, 4500–5000 hours with frequencies 14, 56, 60, 86, 74, 62, 48.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 13.3, Question 5.

## Correct Answer

≈ 3406.98 hours
