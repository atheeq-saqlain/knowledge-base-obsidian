# Modal Lifetime Electrical Components

## Statement

Find the modal lifetime of 225 electrical components: lifetimes 0–20, 20–40, 40–60, 60–80, 80–100, 100–120 hours with frequencies 10, 35, 52, 61, 38, 29.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 13.2, Question 2.

## Correct Answer

65.625 hours
