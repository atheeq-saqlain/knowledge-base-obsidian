# Median Age of Policyholders

## Statement

Calculate the median age of 100 policyholders from the less-than cumulative data: age below 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50, 55, 60 with cumulative frequencies 2, 6, 24, 45, 78, 89, 92, 98, 100.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 13.3, Question 3.

## Correct Answer

≈ 35.76 years
