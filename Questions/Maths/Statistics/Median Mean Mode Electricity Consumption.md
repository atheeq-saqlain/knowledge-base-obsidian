# Median Mean Mode Electricity Consumption

## Statement

Find the median, mean and mode for the monthly power consumption of 68 consumers: consumption (units) 65–85, 85–105, 105–125, 125–145, 145–165, 165–185, 185–205 with frequencies 4, 5, 13, 20, 14, 8, 4.

## Description

Source: KTBS Class 10 Maths Part II, Exercise 13.3, Question 1.

## Correct Answer

Median = 137; mean ≈ 137.06; mode ≈ 135.76
